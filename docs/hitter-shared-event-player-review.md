12 HR across those levels, plus his real short 2022 record. At age nineteen
he has high scouting/draft inputs. Current expected PA stays 351, from 88.7%

wins/600; contribution falls 1.571 to 1.298, closer to -0.340 observed over
208 MLB PA. His actual 69 strikeouts and five HR remain visible. The new
event terms contribute only +0.057, while age contributes +0.908 and several
other terms offset it. This is a smaller optimistic error, not a correct collapse
prediction. Chourio, Wood and Merrill succeeded in his origin-selected peer
set; Anthony had no MLB PA. Pessimism is not generally the right answer.

Alonso is the largest deterioration. In 2018 he had 273 AA PA with fifteen

Edman, Thaiss, Rooker and Lopez are his origin-selected peers, with actual MLB
PA of 349, 164, zero and 402. The 344 coarse matching training players are not
proof that the model recognizes an unusually strong power prospect.

## What the test can and cannot establish

This review completes the test and supports keeping the incumbent, not rejecting
minor or foreign evidence. Shared event production is now nonzero and coherent,
but the second regression remains heavily conditional on context and existing
MLB rate fields. An already predicted future event profile then enters another
batting-value regression; that is not a necessary mathematical conversion of
events to value. The saved-head directions and actual training-weight mix are
recorded as a no-fit diagnosis. Actual never-debut training support is 376–1047
people per cell but only 5.8–7.2% of training PA weight. In five of 35 foreign
heads, a coherent single-to-strikeout exchange increases the forecast; that is
a reasonability warning conditional on unchanged context, not a causal claim
or proof of physical impossibility. All HR-for-other directions are positive.
These diagnostics do not prove a different forecast would win.

No further algorithm or coefficient sweep follows this loss. The justified
remaining issue is whether reliable predicted MLB event probabilities are being
validated and converted to their defined run value, rather than judged through
an additional largely MLB-trained scalar regression that can suppress their
information. Any test of that issue needs its own fixed contract and sample-
uncertainty limits. Do not tune to the names above or open 2026 before freezing.
