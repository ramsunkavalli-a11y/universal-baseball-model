# Thirteen player walks through source history and actual training support

Reviewed 2026-10-05. All thirteen fixed origins and their five outcome-blind
training peers were read. Annual team/level counts, foreign history, actual saved
paths, unchanged forecasts and both support views are in the private
`hitter-route-support-diagnostic/full-player-walks.json`. An origin predicts the
following MLB season, not eventual ability or six years of value. No player was
assigned a custom forecast. The source-boundary assertion and its separately
sealed recovery are recorded in [the execution note](hitter-route-walk-execution-note.md).

AA PA, but no 2022 MLB PA. Current missingness is explicitly flagged. His saved
participation path includes positive prior first-team work and major linkage,

effects. The resulting 15.35% times 400.38 gives 61.48 PA versus 635 actual.
None of the restriction inputs is used. There are no matching people in the
audited stratum; the broad absent-career count is not star-return support.

major link and roster indicator despite no current MLB PA. These raise his
participation to 92.14%. Signed prior work adds strongly to conditional workload,
while current-work zero reduces it; 398.64 conditional PA gives 367.29 expected
versus 517 actual. Five full/four active profile people is thin but unlike Tatis's
zero. Do not erase this useful existing job-evidence gain or call every absent
player a wrongly suppressed return.

MLB work dominate his 99.06% probability and 549.68 conditional PA, giving

legal restrictions. The captured uncertainty remains unresolved, not a retroactive
permanent ban. A healthy sporting analogue is not an eligibility analogue.

Belt 2023 retains 381/298/404 MLB PA and 29/8/19 HR. With no major link or roster
indicator, but useful current work and hitting, the research job head gives

This differs from the frozen incumbent's already saved Belt forecast because
it is the complete-employment research anchor, not an edited 2026 forecast.

Acuña 2023 has 360/533/735 PA and 24/15/41 HR, with the roster and current-work
signals appropriately positive. His 99.29% times 593.42 gives 589.19 PA versus
