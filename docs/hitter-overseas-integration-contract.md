# Complete overseas hitter comparison

2026-10-04. Test whether actual overseas professional production improves next
calendar year MLB hitting, playing time and delivered batting contribution when
dated employment and availability are represented consistently. This is the
next comparison in the practical hitter plan, not another context-only fit or
translation tuning exercise. Protected 2026 and current explorers stay unchanged.

## Population and targets

Keep all 30,506 existing evaluation origins and all 63,282 source origins.
Append the 32 qualified hitter or mixed-role source origins from the completed
admission review, using their actual domestic histories. Only origins 2016–2018
and 2021–2024 enter the evaluation; other qualified origins can train when their
outcomes have matured. Report additions separately, never invent old forecasts
of zero for omitted players. Pitcher-only and unknown additions remain excluded
with the existing admission record. Other missing eligible hitters remain a
coverage limitation, not a claim of complete population coverage.

Train each of five whole-player folds on targets no later than the test origin;
exclude target 2020. Korea and Japan's 2020 history remains real, while canceled
US minor-league history remains explicitly missing. Verify exact release cutoffs.
Reconstruct MLB outcomes and complete league totals from the dated 2008–2025
source. Zero participation is zero delivered contribution, not zero hitting
ability. Future-season league averages define relative labels only, never inputs.

## Two fitted models and fixed diagnostic combinations

Both models retain the 251 existing workload input definitions, including actual
level-separated domestic counts, draft evidence, games and dated prospect ranks.
Both receive corrected literal roster, qualified age/position, ordered employment,
scoped medical/nonmedical status, elapsed evidence age and explicit unknowns.
An old activation is evidence, not a guaranteed current MLB job. Named list
clearing does not clear another list. Only current reviewed permanent/deceased
or retirement evidence can force participation to zero. Finite suspension days,
games and dated tentative return reports are distinct inputs, not interchangeable
durations or known actual returns. Sparse status examples remain flagged.

The domestic comparison receives no overseas production or exposure. The
overseas comparison adds per-league historical exposure, missingness, relative
component contrasts and the already reviewed borrowed-stability translation.
Use saved origin-local profiles excluding both the outer test-player fold and
each training player's own fold; never refit translation. Preserve recent MLB
evidence in both models. Translated foreign evidence supplements it, not replaces
it. Foreign park exposure and selection of MLB movers remain limitations.

Each model fits the same fixed histogram gradient boosting participation and
conditional PA heads: 250 iterations, depth 3, minimum leaf 30, learning rate
0.05, L2 10, no early stopping, seed 31, equal-origin weights. Expected PA is
participation probability times conditional PA bounded to [1,800]. Fit one
standardized ridge hitting head per model, alpha 100, to active MLB hitters'
future-season-relative batting wins per 600 PA. Precision weights cap actual PA
at 300 and are normalized to equal total origin weight. This raw-history ridge
does not silently inherit all Statcast/contact/park winners. Physical rate limits
use the known origin environment, not next year's realized environment.

Delivered contribution is expected PA times (rate/600 + origin replacement
reference). This product is a testable mean approximation, not a certified joint
distribution or full WAR. On original rows also report, without new fits,
new PA times saved current talent and saved current PA times new talent. These
isolate head improvements and harms; they do not authorize a post-result blend.
Compare complete candidates with saved current PA/rate/value and public forecasts.

## Checks before any fit and before a decision

Persist every real training/test and active subset preflight before fits, including
distinct people by age, debut, workload, foreign exposure, employment and status,
plus feature-range warnings. Unknown history never becomes observed zero talent.
Keep sparse/extrapolated forecasts in all headline losses. Verify nested profile
exclusions, domestic reconstruction, original labels, addition membership, feature
finiteness and future-row mutation invariance. Hash the contract, code and inputs.

Primary scores use fixed equal-origin row weights: delivered relative batting
contribution RMSE, PA RMSE/MAE/bias, participation Brier/log loss, active conditional
rate RMSE. Also report PA-weighted rate diagnostics, original and added cohort
totals, each origin/stage, non-arrivals, high-workload and young/minor profiles.
Use 2,000 whole-player bootstrap draws, seed 104, retaining the original origin
weights rather than silently renormalizing origins per draw. Intervals are nominal
development evidence because historical outcomes have already been exposed.
The practical plan's public tolerances remain: PA RMSE within 10% and MAE within
15% of Steamer, delivered batting RMSE within 10%, with public environment and
snapshot-date qualifications. No small pooled gain overrides systematic cohort
or baseball failures.

Fixed player walks include the fifteen completed status cases plus Judge, Kurtz,
Kwan and Caminero where eligible, and admitted Ohtani, Suzuki, Yoshida, Lee,
Bogusevic and the eligible ordinary addition. Add each model's largest gain,
harm, false high/low and ordinary case. Trace actual counts through inputs,
head outputs and observed MLB outcomes, with origin-only peers matched on job
evidence as well as exposure. Review major 2021 errors and small mover support.
Scores remain provisional until walks are complete. Separate execution integrity,
profile support, prediction, reasonability and deployment. No model deployment,
six-year/control claim or new testing direction follows automatically.
