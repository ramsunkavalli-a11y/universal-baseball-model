## Judgment and disposition

The source repair passes the chronology and baseball interpretation checks for
these cases: history stays, subsequent observed MLB use is separate, newer
restrictions and hard bans survive, and unsuccessful return/exit profiles remain.
The broad source count includes pitcher-like people and training-only origins;
the relevant scored scope is 89 forecasts for 28 people, not all 155 source rows.

Retain the observation evidence as a source repair, not a promoted predictor.
No predictive loss or confidence interval is available because no model was
fitted. Sparse finite-suspension and parallel-list profiles still lack adequate
matching peers. A future matched historical comparison must correct dependent
timing inputs consistently and show whether this changes PA/value/calibration,
not just a handful of favorable source cases. The completed 2026 evaluation,
all saved forecasts and the explorer remain unchanged.
