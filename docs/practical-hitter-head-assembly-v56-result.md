2026-10-03. Eight actual source-to-fit player reviews complete. No new fits,
weight search, evaluation exclusions or protected 2026. All established MLB
forecasts remain exactly corrected V53.

Combining standardized prospect opportunity and fixed-unit prospect hitting
has essentially the same never-debut offense RMSE (.153978) as retaining the
baseline hitting model with that opportunity. Its nominal MSE interval against
the simpler construction spans -.000355 to +.000367. Lower-minor errors worsen;
upper-minor errors improve slightly. This is not a meaningful rate-model win.

versus 487, but his conditional-PA fit contains offsetting old rookie triples,
HBP and walk terms of roughly -61, +58 and -52 PA. Holliday remains overconfident:
455 PA versus 208 and positive hitting versus strongly negative actual.

Keep corrected baseline hitting. The simpler prospect PA-only model remains
qualified research. Stop repeated scaling/assembly of this over-detailed
readiness representation. A compact opportunity model should use genuinely
relevant readiness evidence without dozens of rare rookie outcome-rate terms.
This is a materially different representation question, not another library
competition or a claim that minor-league production is irrelevant.
