# Same source histories, different future batting learner: sixteen walks

2026-10-05. Thirteen previously reviewed cases were locked before this fit.
The mechanical post-fit rule adds Suzuki 2022 (largest delivered gain versus the
incumbent), Thames 2017 (largest harm) and Nola 2021 (ordinary active case).
Judge 2016 and Tatis 2021 remain false low/high. No unfavorable case is dropped.
These outcome-selected examples are explanatory development evidence, not an
independent confirmation or a player-specific tuning rule.

All source histories were reconstructed and paired with the sealed matrices.
Every scalar forecast replays as translated/shrunk past baseline + fitted
intercept + the sum of recorded feature effects. Fixed-fit removal probes rebuild
the profile and its mass/share inputs but retain history/role/tracking controls;
they are artificial diagnostics, not causal importance or alternative validated
forecasts. Three distinct training-player comparisons are selected using only
stage/debut, foreign presence, age, MLB exposure, draft/scouting and past-profile
distance. Their later failures stay visible. Support counts are coarse profile
cells, not proof of equivalent talent; source-range warnings remain in the walks.

Units below are next-year MLB batting wins above league mean per 600 PA. Actual
hitting is unobserved for a non-arrival. Delivered value adds replacement at
fixed PA; none of this is full WAR or trade value.

## Retained source cases and changed mechanics

**Yordan 2018:** current AA/AAA 379 PA, 20 HR, 92 K plus earlier A/A+ and 57
DSL PA receive the same level graph and 1,200-PA prior as before. The scalar
correction +0.961 lifts the low baseline only to +0.493; age contributes +0.697
and current scout listing +0.237, among other terms. Removing minor production
raises the forecast +0.365. Delivered value stays only 0.282 versus 4.610 actual.
Origin-selected Sanchez/Singleton/Pederson include a non-arrival and poor early
MLB outcomes. The breakout remains missed, not caused by old DSL domination.

**Judge 2016:** 95 MLB PA (four HR, 42 K) and current AAA 410 PA (19 HR, 98 K)
plus older A/AA/AAA yield the unchanged negative starting profile. Direct adds
+0.631, including a +0.354 age effect but a −0.229 draft-class-unknown term;
these are fitted associations, not causal baseball penalties. Removing minors
raises rate +0.542. Direct value 0.907 remains below incumbent 1.093 and actual
8.115. d'Arnaud/Alonso/Taylor peers remain non-superstar future outcomes; older
tracking coverage extrapolation remains. Direct learning alone does not recover
his damage and walks or the large workload miss.

**Kurtz 2024:** 35 A PA with four HR and 15 AA PA with none still form only
4% of the starting pool. Direct adds +0.450; age contributes +0.745, draft rank
+0.093, current scout listing +0.166, offset by intercept and other terms.
Removing minor production lowers rate −0.304, so the small production sample
does help. Direct 0.688 is above count but below incumbent 1.024; 10.2 expected
PA makes delivered 0.043 versus actual 5.724. Brooks Lee/DeLauter no-arrivals and
Zunino's modest first-year hitting do not support a blanket rapid-arrival rule.

**Suzuki 2021:** 1,659 NPB PA produce the same 52.2% mass share and +3.921
local-dominance baseline. Direct subtracts only 0.329, leaving +3.591 versus
actual +1.175. The count-past-HR linear effect is positive +0.164; sparse league
adaptation remains inadequate. Foreign removal changes rate −4.330. Delivered
1.746 versus 2.271 actual looks better than count partly because high hitting
offsets low PA. There is no incumbent; zero active exact peers and unsuccessful
Rosario/Nakajima/Meneses comparisons remain support warnings, not absence of all
foreign training or a reason to assign zero ability.

**Lee 2024:** 158 MLB PA and weighted KBO 685.8 PA still start at +1.840.

removal lowers rate −2.018. Value rises to 0.840 at unchanged low expected PA,

**Yoshida 2024:** 885 weighted MLB PA, eight AAA PA and 304.8 weighted NPB PA

Delivered 2.537 exceeds actual 0.489 because both talent and PA point high.
Aoki/Suzuki successful peers and Brosseau's absence do not validate the optimism.

**Maitan 2017:** 176 rookie PA with two HR/49 K produce baseline −1.132.

## Three added walks, not just error-table names

**Suzuki 2022, largest gain:** cutoff-known first MLB season has 446 PA, 14
HR, 110 K, 39 unintentional walks and eleven AAA PA. NPB 2021/2020 have 533/514
PA with 38/25 HR; recency weighting gives 734.8 foreign PA versus 446 MLB and
eleven minor PA. Own NPB HR probability about 6.23% is measured against a local
reference about 2.21%, then reanchored before the shared prior; it is not a proven
MLB equivalency. Baseline +2.577 plus scalar correction +0.142 produces +2.719,
versus incumbent +0.160 and actual +1.997. Current workload contributes +0.197;
intercept and other terms largely offset. Foreign removal lowers the rate
−2.331, minor removal only −0.054: Japanese history, not eleven AAA PA, drives
the change. At 406 expected versus 583 actual PA, delivered value 3.113 is much
closer than old 1.380 to actual 3.766, although hitting overshoots. Coarse support
is three full/two active people. Origin-selected Tsutsugo 2021 produces poor
193-PA hitting; Evans and Clark 2014 do not appear. This gain is real for the row,
but does not make sparse foreign adaptation reliable.

**Thames 2017, largest harm:** known MLB return has 551 PA, 31 HR, 163 K and
70 unintentional walks. KBO 2016/2015 have 529/595 PA and 40/47 HR, contributing
780.2 weighted PA. Smoothed own KBO HR probability about 7.75% is compared with
local 2.56%, reanchored to roughly 9.78% before pooling. The +3.150 baseline
gets only −0.078 scalar correction, leaving +3.072 versus incumbent +0.370 and
actual +0.622. Age contributes −0.346 but past HR and workload terms partly
offset it. Foreign removal lowers rate −2.476 to +0.596; this fixed-fit probe is
explanatory, not a validated replacement forecast. At 444 expected versus 278
actual PA, value 3.642 is too high versus actual 1.144. Exact coarse support is
zero full/active; selected Kang 2015 succeeds, Park 2016 does not appear and Kim
2016 struggles. The local-star baseline can transfer too much past foreign
dominance even after a full recent MLB season. The model has no right to know a
future decline, but sparse context adaptation remains a genuine integration gap.

**Nola 2021, ordinary:** current MLB 194 PA with two HR/19 K coexist with
2020 MLB 184 PA (seven HR), 2019 MLB 267 PA (ten HR), current AAA 39 PA and older
AAA 229 PA. The 2020 shortened source season remains observed evidence, not an
invented full-season count. Level translation/prior start at +0.611. Scalar
correction −0.952 includes age −0.484 and catcher-position association −0.250,
plus tracking and other terms; this is batting prediction, not credited catcher
defense. Direct −0.341 remains above actual −0.925, yet delivered 0.631 almost
matches actual 0.632 because expected PA is only 246 versus 397. Removing minor
evidence lowers rate −0.140. Coarse support 305 full/179 active does not make
these exact peers: Flaherty/Robinson have poor small-sample futures and Clint
Robinson does not appear. The ordinary near-exact value is another cancellation,
not proof its parts are calibrated.

## Judgment

Every required category and all original cases have been walked through. Direct
learning improves the failed count model's hitting score, but not delivered
accuracy against the incumbent; mature-MLB losses, breakouts and weak foreign
context remain. The actual input/model traces support reviewing lost MLB detail
next, not declaring all detailed outcomes useless or fitting new algorithms to
avoid the source question. Walkthrough status is complete; candidate adoption
and sufficient profile support are not approved.
