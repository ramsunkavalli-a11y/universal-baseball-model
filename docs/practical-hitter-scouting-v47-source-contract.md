# Historical scouting evidence for hitter readiness

2026-10-03. Source work before any predictive fits. The question is whether dated
scouting information can distinguish elite fast movers from ordinary minor
leaguers beyond the working model's age, level, batting counts and draft pick.
Do not hand-boost Kurtz, Volpe, Bellinger or any known successful prospect.

## Evidence to inspect

Inspect the original publisher's historical MLB Pipeline top lists for 2011–2024
and the public Trouble with the Curve research dataset. First capture a small
sample of publisher pages and the dataset schema, with URL, retrieval time and
content hash. Current organization, age or level on a historical ranking page
may be live biography fields. Report year does not alone establish publication
date or that tool grades are from that vintage. Identify precisely which fields
are historical and which are not. No 2026 results or new college data.

The [research paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.12622v1) provides scouting text
and numerical grades, but its debut-by-age-24 demonstration is not our target.
We will not import its outcome labels, age censoring or random text splits.
[KATOH's comparison](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/an-improved-katoh-top-100-list/)
supports testing scouting alongside a stats-only anchor; it does not prove
scouting must improve our model. Future value is not next year's batting rate,
nor a guarantee of playing time.

## Before source acceptance

Require stable MLBAM identity or a documented unique identifier bridge, report
season, source publication/as-of provenance and full-list/coverage information.
Reconcile repeated identities and revisions rather than summing ranks. Unknown
reports remain missing. Absence can mean not ranked only in a certified complete
list; unknown team coverage cannot become a low grade. Retain non-arrivals and
failed prospects. A contemporary capture of a historical page is retrospective
evidence, not an archived publication-time capture.

Walk through Bellinger, Judge, Volpe, Kurtz, one unranked entrant and at least two
ranked disappointments if present. Source reviews must compare actual report
fields to cutoff-known production and any independently dated publisher evidence.
They establish source eligibility, not predictive success. No fitted forecast
change until the source review is complete.

## Bound the next decision

If the sources cannot establish field vintage, retain the diagnostic and move
to a verified alternative such as actual minor-league promotion/starting-use
history. Do not spend days repairing a current ranking into fake historical
grades. If usable coverage exists, declare one matched comparison before fits:
same broad evaluation identities, chronological held-player folds and working
anchors, with a small scouting rank/grade/readiness extension. Preserve the
ordinary unscouted population and source-missing flags. Audit actual distinct-
player scouting support in each training fold. No library or parameter sweep.

The final disposition must include future MLB PA, batting-plus-replacement
contribution, public matches, origin/stage totals, arrival/false-high/false-low
cases and a complete post-test player walkthrough. Improvements in a rare
prospect group alone cannot certify the whole hitter model or career value.

## Source amendment before fitting

The publisher pages return a complete 50-name list for 2011, complete 100-name
lists for 2012–2019 and 2022–2024, and only ranks 1–99 for 2020 and 2021.
Preserve verified positive ranks in those two partial lists, but absence remains
unknown rather than an unranked zero. Source capacity and availability are
explicit. Other incomplete/duplicate lists still fail closed. The research
dataset has discordant duplicate profiles and ends in 2019; no text, grades,
ETA, future labels or biographies from it enter this first comparison. Publisher
biographies also mix report titles and live fields. The first predictive test,
if authorized by the source review, will use only historical rank identity,
capacity and coverage, not a claim of fully verified historical tool grades.
