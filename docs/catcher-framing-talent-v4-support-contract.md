# Check usable history for catcher framing talent

2026-10-06, before any framing fit. Use only the certified modern 2018–2025
received-pitch/run source. Keep 2016–2017 modern placeholders quarantined;
do not silently splice legacy framing or use 2026 outcomes.

Include every player with at least one measured received pitch in the preceding
three calendar years at each origin 2018–2024 except 2020. Estimate later MLB
framing quality from the following three calendar years, as pooled native
framing runs per 1,000 received non-swing pitches. A usable quality label needs
a complete window by 2025, at least two measured seasons and 6,000 total pitches.
Native positive catcher exposure without a matching pitch measurement makes
the label incomplete. Non-arrivals, converts and short future samples have
unknown quality, not measured zero talent. Keep all eligible origins in coverage.

choosing a learning contract. Count distinct people, mature origins, ages,
weighted pitch exposure (<1,000, 1,000–5,999, 6,000+), age/exposure intersections
and left-truncated histories. Retain measured 2020 MLB observations at their
actual pitch exposure; never substitute the canceled minor season.

Raleigh and Bailey at 2022 where eligible, plus two smallest origin exposures.

recency weights 1, 0.5, 0.25. That calculation is a support/source baseline,
not a fitted, validated talent claim.
