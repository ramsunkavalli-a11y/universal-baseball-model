2026-10-05. The initial 2025 source audit is preserved. Its two denominator
residuals have been traced through official game logs and actual game feeds.
Neither is an unexplained lost tracking row. All four hit types already match
for every player.

Campbell's April 15 interference out, game 778319, PA 6, has no in-play pitch
and no Savant contact. Subtract this one non-contact AB when reconciling normal
physical contacts, as in the reviewed historical boundary.

Canzone's September 18 grounder, game 776272, PA 49, has a 109 mph speed and
zero-degree angle in both sources. The official play awards first after a shift
violation, without an AB. A 2025 shift-violation award produces an error but no
at-bat. See [MLB's January rule announcement](https://www.mlb.com/news/mlb-shift-baserunning-rules-changes-2025).

Keep that physical measurement under the unchanged tracking definition; do not
discard it to force a simple AB-based equality. Add the one independently
documented physical-contact award to the comparable official denominator.
Annotate the contact as an award without an AB, not an ordinary batting out or
evidence that its field-error result represents hitting skill. No event-count,
target, forecast, model setting or prior historical receipt changes here.

The corrected source equality is checked separately for every player:
AB minus K plus SF plus SH, minus documented non-contact AB, plus documented
physical-contact awards without AB. Unexplained residuals would still block use.
This amendment permits source reconciliation only, not automatic model approval.
