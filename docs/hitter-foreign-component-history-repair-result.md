# Matching calibration histories fixes the design but not the compressed forecasts

2026-10-04. The [history amendment](hitter-foreign-component-history-amendment.md)
is executed and independently checked. Calibration now receives the same kind
of three-year overseas history as prediction. All mover identities, target
counts, weights, fold exclusions, references and model settings are unchanged.
The original failed adjustment remains preserved. The corrected fit is still
not approved as the main hitting estimate.

## Same comparison after the repair

The corrected preparation has the same 210 chronological cells, 1,680 component
fits and 3,205 outer-fold profiles. All 2,230 calibration histories reconstruct
from reviewed raw counts with no observation after the mover's source year.
Independent checks verify every constrained optimum and profile, with all
target/weight/source-prediction definitions matched to the original. Nineteen
focused tests and the 31-file protected forecast check pass.

The repair changes fitted coefficients, not the players' actual prediction
histories. It does not materially restore contact or power separation in the
important cases below. That means the first failure cannot be attributed solely
to the one-season versus three-season mismatch. Selected small samples and the
strong compression of the affine construction remain substantive limits.

## What is retained and what comes next

Retain the corrected history definition, complete league references and exact
source/count adapter. Withhold both affine point-estimate versions as standalone
talent replacements. This is a failed construction with a repaired methodological
issue, not evidence against adding overseas production. No public benchmark
improvement, full WAR completion or new arrival probability is claimed.

Before the end-to-end fit, retain actual league-relative K, BB, hit and power
profiles and their observed exposure so this compressed estimate cannot erase
the evidence. A regularized component mapping that borrows rate stability from
the larger domestic history is a justified alternative to estimating all
predictive reliability from twenty-seven selected overseas movers. It must be
locked before fitting and checked on the same cases, not tuned to Lee/Ohtani.
Finish the already required finite-status and qualified-addition reconciliation
in the same integration plan. Protected 2026 and the existing explorer remain
unchanged; the broad practical hitter goal is still open.
