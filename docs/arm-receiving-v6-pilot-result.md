# Arm and first base receiving source checks

The completed-season pilot supplies genuine opportunities for both components.
All 350 first-base records reconcile exactly with native receiving runs. Of 843
arm records, 841 reconcile; Matt Reynolds in 2022 and Ryan Vilade in 2025 remain
quarantined. Switching the team-only request off leaves the pilot values
unchanged, so it does not explain those discrepancies. They are not repaired.

## What the player records actually measure

Arm opportunities include runners holding, advancing or being thrown out, rather
than just assists. Judge's 2022 record has 278 opportunities and +2.638 adjusted
runs; Happ has 365 and -0.985; Castellanos has 302 and -2.270. Kiermaier's +2.150
runs come from only 95 opportunities. These are observed contributions, not
automatically reliable talent estimates.

Betts's 2022 total is +1.864 runs across 248 opportunities, with both RF and 2B
credit in the native ledger. In 2025 he has three opportunities and +0.199 runs
across mixed position exposure. Assigning either whole-player rate exclusively
to outfield skill would mix numerator and denominator definitions. Only 420 of
the 843 pilot records have reconciled values and exclusively outfield exposure.
The other records remain available with explicit scope; they do not become zero
outfield skill. Two one-opportunity infield records in 2022 yield raw rates as
high as +19.228 runs per 100 chances, illustrating why raw rates need shrinkage.

First-base receiving is separate from fielding ground balls. Olson's 2022 record
has 731 received throws, 703 outs and 697.140 expected outs: +5.860 outs above
expectation, or +4.395 native runs. Six throw-category counts sum to the total,
and their adjusted outs sum to the same numerator. Freeman has +4.164 runs in
2022 and +5.716 in 2025. Walker changes from -0.933 to +5.284; this demonstrates
variation to be tested, not permission to cherry-pick his good season. Hoskins
changes from -2.082 to -0.127. Received-throw difficulty is already part of this
measurement; upstream thrower credits are not awarded to the receiver.

Twenty-nine focal records and 87 opportunity-selected peers are traced in the
[source walkthrough](../reports/model-evidence/arm-receiving-v6/pilot-player-walkthrough.json).
Kiermaier has no eligible 2025 record; no zero-quality outcome is invented.
Eight focused unit checks pass. This is source qualification, not a forecast
accuracy result or deployment approval.

## Next comparison scope

Extend the same method through arm 2016–2025 and receiving 2021–2025. Quarantine
every additional native mismatch rather than raising tolerance. Keep all-position
arm behavior distinct from isolated outfield quality, and preserve mixed-position
players for contribution accounting. Before fitting, count complete later MLB
quality windows and mature, held-player training support. First-base receiving
has much shorter history and may not support a learned age model.

Any future small-sample recipe must reduce the actual performance signal, not
merely attach a reliability feature. No frozen forecasts, explorer content or
2026 results change at this checkpoint.
