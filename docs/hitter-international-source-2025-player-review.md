# What the recovered overseas season adds for eight players

2026-10-05. These are source reviews, not eight newly scored MLB projections.
All cases use data through December 31, 2025. The fixed cases are Murakami,
Okamoto, Austin, Song and Wisdom. Ordinary positive-PA cases are the lowest
source ID without an MLBAM key in each league; the zero-PA control is the
lowest NPB source ID. No future outcome enters the selection or peer rule.

## How the calculation works

Each review selects the player's exact NPB or KBO source key, including players
without MLB keys. Unknown identities are not pooled together. Annual counts
are summed over 2023–2024, then over 2023–2025. The latter is a three-year
subtotal, not a same-length window or a new recency scheme. No park correction,
league translation, age adjustment, shrinkage or forecast model is applied.
Rates are raw counts divided by PA. Singles are hits minus extra-base hits;
unintentional walks are BB minus IBB. The eight exclusive event counts sum to
PA; the remainder is called other, not ordinary outs.

Three peers per case minimize differences in current-year PA and age within the
same source league. Missing DOB incurs a distance penalty. This is a check of
source exposure and sample size, not evidence that the peers share position,
talent or MLB jobs. Their IDs/counts and all intermediate event counts/rates
are retained in the bounded receipt. Future-record mutation leaves every
cutoff calculation unchanged. No fitted forecast, observed future error,
gain/deterioration or predictive interval exists for this source-only test.

## Munetaka Murakami

NPB source 41845136; MLBAM 808959. At the cutoff he is about 26. His 2023 and
2024 records contain 597 and 610 PA, 31 and 33 homers, and 168 and 180 strikeouts.
The recovered 2025 record adds 224 PA, 22 HR and 64 K in 56 games. The subtotal
becomes 1,431 PA, 86 HR and 412 K rather than 1,207 PA, 64 HR and 348 K.

Raw HR/PA rises from 5.30% to 6.01%; K/PA barely changes, 28.83% to 28.79%.
The added season supplies substantial power evidence but a shorter workload.
It does not by itself say why he played fewer games or how much that power
will translate to MLB. The three age/PA peers have 249–274 PA and 5, 11 and
5 HR, so similar sample size clearly does not imply similar hitting.

## Kazuma Okamoto

NPB source 11515130; MLBAM 672960. At about age 29.5, his 2023–2024 record is
1,200 PA, 68 HR and 208 K. In 2025 he adds 293 PA, 15 HR and 33 K in 69 games.
The new subtotal is 1,493 PA, 83 HR and 241 K. K/PA falls from 17.33% to 16.14%,
while HR/PA changes little, 5.67% to 5.56%.

This evidence differs from Murakami's: lower strikeout rates, different age and
workload. A country-wide bonus would miss that distinction. The peers have
281–332 PA, 0–8 HR and 44–76 K. Neither these counts nor a static MLB key
establish December MLB employment; later signings cannot be inserted into an
earlier population as if known then.

## Tyler Austin

NPB source 73375151; MLBAM 592122. At about age 34.3, his 2023 record has only
54 PA, followed by 445 PA and 25 HR in 2024. The 2025 addition is 246 PA,
11 HR and 45 K in 65 games. The subtotal grows from 499 PA, 25 HR and 101 K
to 745 PA, 36 HR and 146 K. Raw HR/PA moves from 5.01% to 4.83% and K/PA
from 20.24% to 19.60%.

That is positive batting evidence with uneven recent opportunity, not proof of
future availability. Age and prior MLB experience would matter in an actual
forecast; neither is estimated here. Three similarly exposed peers have
240–276 PA but 3, 13 and 20 HR. A missing domestic row must not erase overseas
production, while strong overseas production must not guarantee an MLB job.

## Sung Mun Song

1,040 PA, 24 HR and 120 K. The new year adds 646 PA, 26 HR and 96 K across

## Source disposition

The eight reviews are complete for source mechanics and baseball interpretation.
They identify real missing last-season evidence and preserve ordinary, traded,
unmapped and zero-PA cases. They do not establish improved MLB predictions.
Identity, historical position, employment and park exposure remain separately
qualified inputs. All forecast and future-error fields are explicitly absent.
