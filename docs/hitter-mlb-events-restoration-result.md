# Separate MLB components improve the matched model but do not finish the repair

2026-10-05. The fixed seven-input comparison and seventeen player walkthroughs
are complete. Restoring separate MLB components improves both primary errors
over the same learner with four batting summaries. The paired development
intervals favor that addition. The incumbent still has slightly lower overall
delivered error, so the declared replacement requirement is not met. Keep the
incumbent and existing explorer; retain this as a useful research result.

This is not evidence that more batting information is useless. The controlled
addition helps. Nor is it proof of a satisfactory integrated player-value model:
mature MLB, non-arrivals, foreign transfer, prospect arrival and major player
misses still need attention. No new fitted experiment was chosen before these walks.

## Exact comparison and scope

The [prospective contract](hitter-mlb-events-restoration-contract.md) adds only
seven original pooled MLB rates to the completed four-summary direct-rate model.
The [source audit](hitter-mlb-events-source-audit-result.md) independently verifies
counts, denominators, old priors and all five immutable matrices. The
[pre-fit support review](hitter-mlb-events-restoration-source-review.md) persists
105 extended-feature preflight checks before all 105 fixed Ridge fits.

The 123/132/186-feature routes keep the four quality summaries, common past
baseline, alpha=100, source prior, fixed units, training weights, chronology,
held-player folds, labels, eligibility, route choice and expected PA unchanged.
Three-season recency is 1/.8/.6. The restored rate inputs keep their old
100-opportunity prior and fixed centers, unlike the 1200-PA quality/common prior.
This tests restoration of both component detail and that reliability convention;
it does not establish an optimal prior or park-neutral MLB skill.

All original 30,506 forecasts and thirteen separate additions remain. All prior
forecast columns are preserved exactly. Every fitted head replays; actual labels
reconstruct from dated MLB counts and ten headline equations independently agree.
No scalar event likelihood is invented. Outcomes end in 2025; no 2026 evaluation,
frozen forecast, production artifact or explorer changes.

The target is next calendar year's MLB batting wins above its league mean per
600 PA, conditional on playing. Delivered contribution adds the existing
replacement term at unchanged expected PA and includes non-arrivals with actual
zero contribution but no observed zero hitting ability. It is not full WAR,
lifetime success, club-control years or trade value. Shortened 2020 MLB sources
retain actual precision; canceled MiLB and existing 2021 coverage qualifications remain.

## What the actual players show

The [seventeen walks](hitter-mlb-events-restoration-player-review.md) retain all
fixed cases and unchanged origin-selected peers, failures included. Mechanical
extremes and the ordinary case are already among them: Suzuki 2022 is the largest
incumbent gain, Thames 2017 the largest harm, Judge 2016 the false low, Tatis
2021 the false high and France 2018 the ordinary case. These outcome-selected
roles are diagnostics, not independent confirmation.

Misner's fifteen-PA debut has ten strikeouts. Under the old 100-opportunity prior,
K becomes 28.696%; that coordinate contributes −0.09146 and the seven terms
−0.12428 in total. Relearning older coefficients adds +0.01209. The forecast
moves −1.959 to −2.072 against actual −2.026, improving both errors versus the
matched model. This is a concrete useful component correction, not just a name.

Perdomo's low K and good walk rate add +0.12158 and +0.04737, offset by other
terms. Older coefficient changes subtract −0.13907, so the seven-component
forecast rises only −0.693 to −0.677 against actual +2.728. The contact profile
is useful, but the major breakout miss persists. Judge's rookie poor-debut K
term makes an already low estimate slightly lower; his established forecast also
falls slightly despite positive walk/power terms because other coefficients change.

Lee's low MLB K lifts an already optimistic hitting estimate +1.311 to +1.432
against +0.463 actual. Delivered error improves only because expected 154 PA
is far below 617 actual. Yoshida's low K also raises optimism before a decline,
worsening both errors. Their new joint profiles have no active support. France's
nearly exact delivered forecast still combines too little PA and too much hitting.
These are reasons not to call this a completed player-value solution.

Negative fitted BABIP coefficients are residual corrections conditional on the
common batting baseline, MLB-quality fields and other covariates. They are not
claims that getting a hit is bad or causal estimates of a BABIP change. An
overlapping aggregate can make a coefficient unintuitive; exact traces explain
the arithmetic but do not by themselves certify a sensible causal or predictive
interpretation. The walkthrough preserves those terms rather than hiding them.

The matched public set has 2,627 forecasts and 2,088 active cases. Under
future-relative labels, incumbent hitting RMSE is 1.66475 versus components

components 1.71254, Steamer 1.77459 and ZiPS 1.75336. Reference conventions

New joint profiles have 1,376 forecasts with zero active training people and
11,774 with fewer than twenty, including zero. Eighty-four range-warning records
affect 34 distinct forecasts. These are retained, not excluded. None of the
seventeen fixed cases has a warning on the seven added coordinates, but several
have sparse or absent joint support. No physical rate-envelope violations occur.
Execution passes are not support, predictive or deployment approval. Older
source/graph/tracking and history-left-truncation qualifications remain.

## Decision and next diagnosis

Retain the incumbent under the prospective rule. Preserve the useful matched
component result without a favorable subgroup blend, new prior search or more
algorithm sweeps. Before deciding on another fit, decompose the saved remaining
incumbent delivered-error difference by origin-known MLB exposure and foreign
history, including hitting/workload interaction and non-arrivals. That will show
whether the remaining gap is mature domestic evidence, source transfer or error
cancellation, rather than assuming the earlier count-model explanation still
applies after these repairs. This is diagnosis of existing forecasts, not a
license to fit a post-hoc subgroup fix or tune on 2026. The full hitter goal stays active.
