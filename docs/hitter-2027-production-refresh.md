# Refresh the existing hitter forecast for 2027

This is a production-year refresh of the reviewed hitter model, not a new
algorithm contest or an accuracy-improvement claim. As of October 9, 2026, the
2026 season supplies current predictors and one additional completed training
season. The previously frozen 2026 forecast and its evaluation stay unchanged.

Keep the five player-held folds, the selected histogram participation/workload
models, Ridge batting models and their exact feature definitions/settings.
Append the original 4,030-player 2025-origin population with certified 2026
outcomes; do not select that population using who succeeded in 2026. Historical
training membership and the exclusion of target 2020 remain unchanged. Each
head excludes the forecast players' entire held fold. Prospect translation uses
its own origin and excludes that same fold. Apply the reviewed fixed small-sample
repair to prospect event inputs in both training and prediction.

The forecast target is 2027 MLB participation, PA if active, expected PA, and
batting wins above average per 600 PA in the existing fixed 10-runs-per-win
units. Their combination with replacement is still not full WAR. The later
component ledger converts these batting units back to runs before combining
with running, fielding, position and one common runs-per-win denominator.

Before fitting all 25 heads, verify source receipts, no future labels, identical
feature definitions, unique identities, complete labels, player exclusion,
finite inputs and distinct-player support by stage, age, career, workload and
position. Retain unsupported profiles and flag them. The new population includes
2026 hitters, current roster nonpitchers and the old fixed population, including
exits. New no-history players remain identified rather than silently omitted.

The build predates the 2027 preseason ranking list. Its absence is missing, not
an unranked observation; published older rankings keep their actual lag. This
early-offseason vintage is a release qualification. Unresolved injuries and
temporary restrictions do not become arbitrary season-long zero forecasts.
Apply only the established reversible retirement/permanent-ineligibility rules
to participation, never to talent.

Save and replay every fitted head. Walk the eight fixed source-review players
and their outcome-blind peers through the actual head predictions, routing,
PA multiplication and batting accounting; examine the largest changes and
extreme forecasts too. There are no observed 2027 outcomes, so reasonability
review is not prospective accuracy validation. Historical component evidence,
full-WAR benchmark checks and current rights valuation remain separate release
gates under the finalization plan. Do not publish this intermediate batting
table as the finished player-value explorer.
