# Prospect experience and playing time diagnosis

2026-10-05. This is a read-only diagnosis of saved forecasts, not a new model
test. The completed 2026 result is already exposed. It can illustrate a failure
but cannot select, tune or validate a replacement. Both frozen packages and all
saved predictions remain unchanged.

## Question and prior evidence

Does the current playing-time model actually lose substantial level experience
when a player makes a brief promotion? Where do its prospect PA errors come
from: the workload conditional on arriving, probability assigned to eventual
arrivals, or PA allocated to non-arrivals?

The older primary-level exposure test already tested promotion cameos in a
different pipeline. The recent matched prospect-only conditional-head test
failed. The available-season history comparison improved arrival scores after
the COVID cancellation, without a material delivered-value win. Do not rerun
these recipes, introduce a blanket prospect boost or treat them as untested.

## Fixed records and measurements

Retain all 30,506 historical forecasts at origins 2016–18 and 2021–24. The main
diagnosis includes all 24,199 never-debut records, not just eventual MLB players.
Keep the saved chronological, held-player memberships, probabilities,
conditional PA and translated prospect hitting rates. Historical value uses the
existing common-origin batting-plus-replacement ledger, not full WAR. Report
2026 separately from history, using its already certified frozen-cohort result.
Do not pool the two vintages or silently equate their value ledgers.

Verify each of the three annual level-PA input slots against the saved annual
source counts for the full historical input frame. Missing source records yield
zero exposure only within this already reviewed source panel. This check does
not establish universal international coverage. Mexican-league PA remains
separate. Combine DSL and the several rookie-league buckets only for this broad
exposure diagnosis; preserve their separate raw source counts in player walks.
An annual season-level stint file cannot establish the date of a promotion.

Define primary level as the affiliated level with most origin-year PA, with a
higher-level tie break. Highest level means any positive origin-year PA.
Predeclare highest-level exposure bands of 1–29, 30–199 and at least 200 PA.
These are descriptive bins, not tuned reliability cutoffs or permission to
change eligibility. Zero affiliated exposure is a separate retained group.
Summarize highest-versus-primary agreement, each exposure band, origin year and
preseason rank band. Cross exposure with rank only; no exhaustive subgroup hunt.
Count distinct people as well as records and show annual sign consistency.

Use exact retrospective PA accounting: conditional-forecast error among actual
arrivals minus their probability discount plus expected PA allocated to actual
non-arrivals equals total PA error. This identity is not a causal explanation;
actual arrivals are unknowable at forecast time. A conditional mean that looks
right in aggregate can still badly misallocate individual workloads.

## Player and model review

Fixed historical cases are Kurtz 2024, Langford 2023, Julio Rodriguez 2021,
Acuna 2017, Alonso 2018, Holliday 2023 and Cartaya 2023, when eligible. Add the
largest positive and negative saved expected-PA error among promotion-cameo
records and one ordinary cameo, with deterministic row-ID tie breaking.
2026 Made, De Vries, Rumfield and McGonigle are explanatory checks only.

For each case persist actual three-season level counts, actual PA inputs,
ranking evidence, probability, conditional/expected PA, hitting and value,
outcomes and an exact replay of both saved PA heads. Select up to three peers
without looking at outcomes: same origin, never-debut status, rank band, primary
and highest levels and age within two years. Distance uses origin-known age,
exposure, position and rank. Do not substitute unranked peers for an elite
prospect when support is absent. Count distinct matching training people from
each actual full and active head membership; retain unsupported cases.

## Allowed decision

If level-specific PA is present and reconciles, a coarse explorer stage label
alone is not a missing-feature bug. Model underuse is a hypothesis, not proved
by an anecdote or a path attribution. If the errors vary by year/profile, do
not fix them by a universal conditional-PA increase. Save an interpretable
player review before any next experiment. This diagnosis may close a proposed
repeat and prioritize source coverage or the public benchmark; it cannot
establish a predictive improvement or approve deployment.
