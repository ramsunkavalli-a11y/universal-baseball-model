# Prospect development-path result

Status: narrowly promoted for pitcher established-role probability; rejected for the
other five hitter/pitcher outcome combinations.

The candidate adds four cutoff-safe facts from official affiliated statistics to the
accepted level-exposure model: change in workload-weighted level from the prior active
season, seasons since affiliated activity, total historical PA/BF, and number of
active affiliated seasons.

For pitcher established-role probability, pooled Brier improved from 0.00899 to
0.00889 and log loss from 0.03900 to 0.03816. Neither score worsened in any of the
three time-ordered folds. The simpler level-exposure model had a marginally lower
pooled Brier (0.00888), while the development path had lower log loss and was selected
under the predeclared proper-score gate.

The development path was not selected for hitter arrival, meaningful role, or
established role, nor for pitcher arrival or meaningful role. Those outcomes retain
the simpler level-exposure model (with hitter established role retaining core).

No outside FV, organization depth, player exception, or future information is used.
The feature is not evidence for a manual promotion bonus; it is a small probability
calibration improvement learned from earlier cohorts.
