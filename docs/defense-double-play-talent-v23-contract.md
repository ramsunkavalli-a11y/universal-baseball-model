## Target and fixed population

Use the exact MLB-history origin-position identities and three-calendar-year
same-position quality labels saved in the source review. The primary cutoff is

year. Eligibility is origin-known range-history exposure, not future DP success.
Quality requires 1,500 certified outs over at least two measured seasons and no

unknown labels and every eligible forecast; non-arrivals are not bad defenders.

The target is **adjusted DP runs per 500 defensive innings under later observed

validated average-defense grade. Lower-level talent and longer paths stay open.

## One fixed comparison

Benchmark: zero adjusted DP credit. Candidate: same-position known qualified DP
runs from origin year and the prior two calendar years, weighted 1, 0.5 and 0.25,
divided by similarly weighted native outs **plus 3,000 zero-credit prior outs**,
and multiplied by 1,500. No known qualified history means exactly the benchmark.
The prior and recency are inherited practical assumptions from the transparent
range-history baseline, not DP-specific optimized quantities or proven posterior
uncertainty. No learner, hyperparameter sweep, age regression or cross-position
transfer follows an inconclusive result. The 2020 short MLB season contributes
only actual measured outs, not a full-season extrapolation.

Save raw annual rows, weighted numerators/denominators, reliability, missing
history and model inputs for every forecast. Compute profiles from origin age,
position and weighted DP exposure, not inherited range exposure. Report distinct
earlier measured people in each actual held-ID-fold exclusion with whole target
windows mature by the origin. This is a deterministic rule with no fitted player
references; fold separation audits support rather than creating sham fitted
models. Report sparse intersections and tracking-era mixes. No silent removal
of unsupported forecasts to improve headline scores.

## Scores and baseball checks

Score matched measured positions with equal total weight per person within each
origin. Report RMSE, MAE, signed bias and matched oracle-exposure run totals.
Paired person bootstrap uses 2,000 draws and seed 23023 for 2022, 23022 for 2021.
The primary quality screen requires candidate RMSE lower with a 95 percent
change interval strictly below zero. Primary position groups with at least ten
measured people must not worsen RMSE by more than five percent. Overall MAE must
not worsen. Signed bias magnitude must be no more than the larger of 0.15 runs
per 500 innings or benchmark magnitude plus 0.05. Age and weighted-history bands,
including tiny histories, remain reported even if small.

Quality success alone is not value/deployment success. Even a real small DP gain
is not a complete defense model. If this rule passes, perform a separate matched
unchanged-opportunity delivered-value check; if it fails, retain neutral DP and
record the measurement/context limit without repeatedly retuning the weights.
