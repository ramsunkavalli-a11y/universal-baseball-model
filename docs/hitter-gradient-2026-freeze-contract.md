# Hitter gradient 2026 freeze contract

The development winner is frozen from information ending in 2025. The forecast
universe is every affiliated hitter with at least 30 official 2025 terminal
contacts. The model starts with the frozen contact-only ridge projection and adds
nine outcome-specific histogram-gradient residual corrections using the complete
155-feature `plus_park` family from the feature-ablation result.

The tree settings, feature list, player-cell prior, park prior, source hashes,
forecast hash, and forecast-input hash are recorded in the artifact manifest. No
hyperparameter or feature selection is permitted after this freeze.

The 2025 official terminal-contact source supports exact contact cells, venue,
handedness, HR/non-HR-reach opponent history, hit-composition opponent history, and
park effects through 2025. Because the full 2025 non-contact PA sidecar is not
available, K, UBB, and HBP pitcher residuals are carried forward from their latest
strictly-prior 2024 values. This limitation is recorded rather than filled using
2026 evidence.

No 2026 participant list, playing time, result, park result, or partial-season
outcome may be used to change or score the package until the protected season is
declared complete.
