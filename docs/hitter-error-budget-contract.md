## Population and exact accounting

completed location and offense-source receipts before analysis. Public comparisons
retain the same 2,627 matches through 2025. Actual rate is undefined for zero PA;
do not invent zero talent or a counterfactual active workload for those players.
Public forecasts lack comparable appearance/conditional heads, so decompose only
our own forecast, not supposed internal Steamer or ZiPS components.

Let p be forecast probability of any MLB PA, c forecast PA if active, y actual
PA, a the indicator y>0, g forecast batting-plus-replacement yield per PA, and
t realized yield when active in the common-origin custom units. The identities
are:

- PA error = (p-a)c + a(c-y).
- Contribution error = (p-a)c g + a(c-y)g + a y(g-t).

The terms are participation allocation, active workload and observed production.
For non-arrivals only the first contribution term exists. Keep signed errors,
component squares and every cross term: total squared error is the sum of
squares plus twice the pair products. Also report component times total error;
those additive allocations can be negative and are not causal removable losses.
Cancellation is sum of absolute components minus absolute total error. A small
final error with large cancellation is not a good set of component forecasts.
The ordering uses forecast yield to value workload error; other exact orderings
are possible. No universal blame fraction or single-head repair follows from it.
