## Units and limits

Hitting ability means expected next-year MLB batting wins per 600 PA, relative
to a league-average environment, excluding replacement and all nonbatting value.
Its observed comparison is the saved future-season-relative rate among actual
MLB participants. Zero MLB PA leaves hitting ability unobserved, not zero.

Delivered batting contribution is the saved expected PA times predicted batting
rate plus the origin replacement reference. Its observed label uses the common
origin batting environment, which is deliberately different from the
future-season-relative rate endpoint. Display this distinction; do not derive
observed contribution from the displayed observed rate. Expected PA is a mean,
not a healthy full-season estimate. Totals describe the selected known cohort,
not a complete future roster or a reconciled league-wide WAR budget.

Show matched Steamer PA and converted batting-contribution comparisons, including
the outstanding PA absolute-error gap. Do not label converted public offense
as native WAR superiority or compare differently centered hitting rates without
an explicit compatible conversion. No full WAR, six years of club control,
salary surplus or trade value is estimated by this explorer.
