# Qualify the official Korean batting source

2026-10-04. The NPB collection is complete with documented gaps; KBO remains
missing. This source test asks whether the public official KBO record pages can
supply historical, unfiltered first-team batting counts and structured player
IDs without a member login. It does not fit a projection, translate league
quality or approve hitter eligibility. The practical hitter plan remains in force.

Use the observed public form controls and links on
[official KBO batting records](https://www.koreabaseball.com/Record/Player/HitterBasic/Basic1.aspx).
Check regular-season 2005, 2015 and 2024 against the returned selected year and
series. Inspect both basic-stat groups, displayed qualification and paging.
Do not assume URL parameters set a historical season or a default average
leaderboard includes low-PA players. Follow only actually published public
forms/links, at a low request rate. Do not bypass login, access controls or a
site denial. Non-success responses and selection mismatches are failed probes,
not missing player performance or permission to invent an undocumented API.

Save original bytes, form/request selection, returned status/URL, capture time
and hashes in the ignored private cache. Never overwrite a completed capture or
probe. Preserve qualified failures and corrections before any broad collection.
Raw and bulk statistics are not approved for public redistribution. Only code,
synthetic tests, aggregate receipts and bounded attributed player review belong
in the repo.

Before collecting all years, establish exact headers, integer count definitions,
unique row keys, stable KBO IDs, complete paging and absence of a hidden qualifying
filter. Distinguish hits, XBH, total bases, RBI, sacrifices, BB/IBB, HBP and K;
do not call missing count fields zero. Reconstruct raw counts separately, check
hit/total-base/PA identities where fields suffice, retain unenumerated PA and
undefined rates. Do not equate a lack of returned rows with an absent season.

Source-only fixed cases are Jung Hoo Lee's 2023 input, Ha-Seong Kim's 2020 input,
Hyeseong Kim's 2024 input, Byung-Ho Park's 2015 input and Eric Thames' 2015 input.
These are expected beneficiaries, a different profile and an MLB-to-KBO mover,
not a sample selected by future success. For the historical-season probe use the
lowest published KBO ID among positive-PA 2005 players as an ordinary case.
Confirm ID/name/stat lines and compare them across basic groups where present.
There are no fitted gains/harms or fabricated forecast intermediates. Any claimed
complete collection needs a full source walkthrough first.

MLBAM cross-references are a separate identity task. Chadwick's NPB keys cannot
be assumed to identify KBO players. Full league collection must retain people
with no MLBAM link; future MLB participation cannot select the foreign source
population. Current career pages may supply stable DOB/identity only if needed,
not future professional outcomes or status as model features.

If the source qualifies, append a fixed collection contract for 2005–2024;
enumerate every season/page/stat group and keep pitcher batting/limited samples
explicit. If it fails, report the exact access/selection/coverage issue and use
the existing FanGraphs member-export route when a human login is available.
Protected 2026 outcomes, the frozen forecast and explorers remain unchanged.
