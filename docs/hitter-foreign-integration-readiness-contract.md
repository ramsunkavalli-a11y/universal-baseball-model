## Implications from projection research

[League equivalencies](https://library.fangraphs.com/principles/league-equivalencies/)
translate underlying components rather than equate league averages or nationality.
[Rosenblum's NPB projection discussion](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/creating-a-projection-for-munetaka-murakami/)
illustrates direction-dependent mover selection and small samples. Good overseas
seasons can both reflect talent and favorable variation before a move. The reverse
move samples different players; pooling directions does not automatically remove
selection bias. [Cartwright's Oliver discussion](https://tht.fangraphs.com/why-oliver-loves-yu/)
also separates park/league translation from regression toward a mean. These
principles motivate source and support checks; published modern projections do
not supply model inputs or a validated numerical translation coefficient here.
