## How to read the forecasts

An origin of 2024 predicts 2025, not 2024. Rate is batting wins above the future
MLB event environment per 600 PA. Delivered contribution multiplies that rate
plus the origin replacement reference by expected MLB PA. It is not full WAR.
No-arrival rate is unobserved; absence of an old forecast remains null.

Both arms share participation and conditional PA. The overseas arm adds seven
translated foreign event contrasts; foreign exposure controls are already in
both. A difference between the two complete rate forecasts includes coefficient
refitting, whereas the listed foreign term sum is exact same-fit accounting.
Neither is a causal effect of having played abroad.

Peers use no later outcomes: same origin, debut, dated major link and literal
roster status, then nearest age, MLB PA, professional work and prospect rank.
They are often not genuine foreign-job, medical or position matches. Coarse
support intersections likewise do not certify detailed role or talent support.

## Decision and remaining methodological gap

The current model remains the benchmark. Tiny-source influence is now stable,
and professional activity improves several useful job estimates, but the complete
candidate does not win. Foreign production effects are extremely small under
this fixed common regularization; most foreign rate uplift is generic exposure
and missingness. Minor K also receives positive coefficients in several cells,
which may reflect selected power prospects and correlated features, not the
causal value of striking out. That relation needs scrutiny before calling the
new pooled representation meaningful talent transport.

The [result](hitter-evidence-representation-result.md) records the pooled uncertainty,
year/stage totals and public limitations. Source repair is not forecast validation;
an ordinary product can be lucky; an increased-workload forecast can be sensible
and still lose when talent declines. Keep these distinctions explicit. No deployment,
2026 outcome access, new feature tournament or post-result blending follows this review.
