# Prepare a matched defensive run ledger before testing player value

2026-10-07. The next question is whether reviewed MLB defensive skill improves
delivered runs and expanded player value. First prepare exact native outcomes,
known historical quality and measurement coverage on the already fixed 12,432
forecasts. No new model, coefficient selection or score is part of this source
gate. Position uncertainty and PA remain the reviewed fixed research inputs.

Use seven position-specific range channels, catcher framing/throwing/blocking,
outfield arms and first-base receiving. Range runs already exclude native arm,
DP, receiving and catcher components. Use corrected full catcher sources and
official-scope arm/receiving records. Exclude DP from the upcoming native-value
definition; its older availability is different and the 2024 FanGraphs WAR
revision removes it. Do not add an aggregate fielding total again.

For a future channel with official positional exposure, a measured native
numerator is required. Range needs its existing range-valid position record;
OF arms need known native arm numerators at every actually played OF position.
Keep arm count/quality qualification separate: a mixed-position player can
have an observed OF run numerator but unknown isolated opportunities. Use
the corrected component measurement flags for catcher and receiving channels.
If the official position has no outs, delivered runs are certified zero
opportunity, not a neutral defensive-talent observation. If applicable evidence
is missing or invalid, keep the outcome null and name the missing channel.
Never delete the player or silently sum unknown channels as measured zero.

Prepare each unchanged three-year, 1/.5/.25 history recipe with its previously
fixed shrinkage and actual opportunities. Label no-history quality unknown
even when its fallback mean is zero. Preserve the separately saved range age
calibration and support fields wherever already available; do not fit or apply
it to an unsupported no-history prospect during source preparation.

Join the already reviewed season-relative batting-value ledger by player,
origin and row ID. Verify identical PA and identities. It contains batting
plus replacement in fixed common wins, not full WAR. Its replacement term
must not be added twice. Runs-to-common-wins is ten runs per win for that
existing definition, not a claim of exact published FanGraphs WAR.

The eventual positional calculation must use actual projected exposure,
not a full-season bonus attached to a roster label. FanGraphs' published
schedule is per 1,458 innings, or 4,374 fielding outs; DH starts are distinct
from innings. Lock the precise DH treatment in the integration contract, not
inside this source gate. Baserunning and historical WAR-definition differences
remain separate ledger items before any full-WAR claim.

Save all forecast identities, channel target status, source record keys,
history means/reliability, range calibration availability, complete versus
partial measurement, matched/omitted full-MLB exposure and input hashes.
Walk at least Kiermaier, Castellanos, Witt, Chourio, Álvarez, Varsho, Olson and
Eldridge with origin-known comparison players. Include tiny/unknown and mixed
position cases; inspect observed skill, delivered opportunities and absent
measurements separately. Prepare an independent arithmetic verification before
final source readiness. Do not start an integration fit or report an accuracy
gain from this audit. Frozen forecasts/explorer/2026 selection remain unchanged.
