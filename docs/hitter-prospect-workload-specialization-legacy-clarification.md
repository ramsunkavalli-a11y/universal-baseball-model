# What the older workload training audit establishes

2026-10-04, after actual membership checks and before the new fits. The original
specialization contract and preflight remain unchanged. This clarification
prevents a code-level concern from becoming an unsupported rejection.

The older detailed conditional head trains on both active established hitters
and active first-time entrants. For its 2016 Year-1 fit, 3,719 of 4,399 training
rows already had a debut; 680 had not. Its prospect-only description concerns
where changed forecasts are used, not a prospect-only training population.

The helper does not explicitly exclude query player identities. There is
substantial active-training overlap with established query players, but **zero
overlap with never-debut query players in all twelve origin/horizon cells**.
That result is consistent with label maturity: a player who has not debuted
cannot have an earlier matured positive MLB-PA label. Thus the missing explicit
player exclusion is not evidence that the primary prospect conditional result
was contaminated by those same prospects' positive labels. Neither this check
nor chronology alone certifies all its feature transformations or the separate
participation model.

Do not reject that earlier prospect result because of established-player
overlap. Keep it as qualified prior evidence, with different source/features,
weights and incomplete modern player walkthroughs. The later integrated result
also showed that its workload gain did not establish a complete value gain.
The new matched comparison changes the conditional training population using
today's inputs; it is not a replication of the old rich feature package or
proof that the old model's algorithm was wrong.

The actual membership counts, source/code hashes and interpretation are saved
in `reports/generated/hitter-prospect-workload-specialization/legacy-audit.json`.
No new fit, source, target, forecast route or setting changed because of this
clarification. Its paired experiment and all 140 preflights remain as declared.
