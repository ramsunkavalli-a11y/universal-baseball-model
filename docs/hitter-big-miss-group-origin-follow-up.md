# What to tackle after the departure repair

2026-10-05. Appended review; no changes to sealed group results or plan.

The pooled upper-minor arrival shortage is real, but does not justify a uniform
arrival-probability boost. Inspect the same fixed cohort by origin:

2021 accounts for roughly 73 of the pooled 131 missed expected arrivals. It is
not the only deficient origin, but the two immediately following cohorts already
overpredict arrivals. Their conditional-group workload also differs. Origin
2021's information date is March 18, 2022, not January: comparisons must respect
that timing and the absent 2020 minor-league season. This diagnosis does not
prove COVID caused every error, or that the current prospect model needs a
uniform change learned from those older years.

Better origin-only baseball peers also change interpretation. Kwan's closest
AA/AAA exposure and contact-result peers include Donovan (64 expected PA, 468
actual), Sands (72, 4), and Pereda (3, 0). Reynolds's include Bishop (72, 60),
Jackson (35, 4), and Robinson (12, 0). Performance plus age/level identifies
plausible bets, not certain future stars. The original MLB-only peer distance
cannot be used to conclude these prospects lacked useful minor-league evidence.
The appended peer fields are identical across the five actual source frames;
they do not inadvertently compare different fold-derived definitions.

Next checkpoint, before another fit: audit whether arrival errors concentrate in
low-probability ready prospects, brief-debut classification, or missing
origin-known medical/availability evidence, and whether that persists in recent
cohorts. Preserve actual injury/career stage and the full-history performance
backbone. Use these groups to choose ONE mechanism with a prospective contract,
not a new tournament or a post-hoc cohort multiplier. Test both probability and
delivered value, hold workload and hitting rate fixed for the first arrival-only
contrast, keep lower-level non-arrivals, and inspect named winners and harms.
Recent cohorts cannot be sacrificed for older COVID-era improvement.

Tatis remains a priority unresolved player: the 2023 finite-game budget is
already implemented, but an unconditional medical/role baseline has not earned
replacement of the stronger model. Nor can his 2022 absence be classified as
wholly unknowable at this row's March 18 cutoff: wrist surgery was reported on
March 16. [Dated official report](https://www.mlb.com/news/fernando-tatis-jr-has-wrist-surgery).
That fact does not make the later full-season suspension known in March, and a
reported recovery timetable is not a calibrated healthy-return probability.

Closed: team-record variants; the standalone source-covered medical refit and
its threshold tweaks; blanket old-player/unsigned penalties; cohort-wide
prospect boosts inferred from the pooled arrival total. Keep current supported
facts and stronger outputs; repair a defined mechanism rather than re-running
the same missed names. No new fitted improvement is claimed by this follow-up.
