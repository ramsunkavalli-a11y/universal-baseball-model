# Position changes improve some forecasts but remain a qualified bridge

2026-10-07. The historical defensive-time forecast is about 2.1% more accurate
than PA-scaled prior usage. Supported position uncertainty partly fixes Chourio
and Holliday, but worsens Volpe and other prospects who stay at SS. Retain the
bridge for research integration; it is not a promoted defensive-talent or
full-WAR forecast.

## What was tested

Only position allocation changes. All 12,432 player forecasts, expected PA,
scalar potential fielding outs, DH forecasts and native conversions are fixed.
Earlier held-player training yields distributions of future MLB positions for
each exclusive dominant origin role and prior-MLB status. No-debut players use
that distribution; substantial MLB experience retains more individual history.
C is forbidden without the recipient's own catching evidence. An established
missed-year return uses older MLB fielding rather than a minor rehab role.

These following-season exposure tests are development evidence from already
inspected historical years. They do not identify minor defensive skill or
certify six-year player value. Frozen forecasts and the explorer are unchanged.

## Player placement and limitations

Chourio's predicted CF outs fall from 1,824 to 1,109 while LF/RF rise to 536/251;
actual is LF 2,171/RF 1,450 with no CF. Holliday's 2B increases from 375 to
722 outs against 1,379 actual. Volpe goes the wrong way: despite 127 minor SS
starts in his latest season, SS falls from 2,514 to 1,371 outs against 4,040
actual. The dominant-role group ignores useful individual distinctions.
[Full player review](defense-transition-v10-player-review.md).

Among future defenders with zero current MLB PA, equal-origin normalized
position-share loss improves about 3.1% against the own-repertoire repair,
but remains about 17.1% worse than the broader PA-ratio reference. That separate
score prevents an apparent gain from simply diluting excessive PA across roles.
Young measured defenders are only 4, 3 and 2 people in the three targets.
The age-15–19 delivered-error group passes the two development tolerances but
is 22.3% worse than PA-ratio in the 2025 stress origin. Basallo/Eldridge's PA
and DH exposure remain unresolved; do not waive the actual failures or remove
the players.

Positive native opportunity errors are mixed relative to the prior repair.
Most small changes go the wrong way outside the 2023 catcher channels. This is
not evidence that the reviewed component skill histories failed: they were
not changed or integrated in this comparison.

## Integrity and disposition

All 30,506 source evidence vectors, 446 learned distributions and 12,432
forecasts independently replay. Support remains sparse: 9,614 detailed profiles
have fewer than 20 held-training people, 3,365 have none, and 213 forecasts have
no supported transition group and retain their own repertoire. Unknown roles
stay unallocated, apart from one recovered older-MLB return explicitly traced.
Totals remain close to matched actuals; that alone does not validate placement.

Two execution corrections are preserved, without model retuning: an explicit
numeric matrix for list-valued share scoring was sealed before fit; a total
check was corrected to conserve potential exposure including unallocated outs.
The latter stopped after all 15 means were saved and before any score existed.
Those same means were replayed unchanged, not fitted again. A separate return
review completes an initially partial focal-only diagnostic receipt.

Retain sources, scalar shrinkage, supported position uncertainty and the separate
skill baselines as qualified research. Do not promote a universal allocator or
start another position-fitting loop. Proceed to a matched skill-times-opportunity
and expanded-value comparison, retaining the stronger anchors and showing where
unknown lower-minor skill, mixed-position measurement, dated assignments and
upstream PA limit the answer. No 2026 outcome selection or batting repair occurred.
