# Historical prospect service-path result

**Status:** historical evidence; not yet used in player value.

The official-event cohort contains 2,598 players who debuted from 2009 through 2019. The old value simulator's one-active-season-equals-one-service-year shortcut is not retained.

Median first-year reconstructed service is 28 days; 97.7% are below a full 172-day year.

Against 547 FanGraphs 2025 opening balances, the median absolute error is 524.0 days and 2.6% are within 15 days.

Promotion requires complete active-season coverage and accurate enough service timing. This artifact does not assign FV, controlled WAR, salary or dollar value.
