# Tatis has a working role reconstruction but the reference model is not ready

The known finite-return mechanism now produces an actual fitted reconstruction,
not hypothetical calendar arithmetic: Tatis before 2023 gets 605 expected MLB
PA under the reported spring-ready assumption, rather than the old 61. He
actually received 635. The medical-risk reference is not sufficiently reliable
to promote this as his finished unconditional forecast or as a general comeback
model. Both frozen forecasts and the explorer remain unchanged.

## What produces the number

His prior 546-PA, 42-HR MLB season and normalized 2020 workload remain in the
role estimate. The separate career-role models use current age, prior workload,
hitting quality, employment and medical evidence, rather than routing him from
zero current MLB PA and an AA rehabilitation stint toward non-arrival. Their
training players have no pending legal restriction; future exits are retained.

The dated medical expectation permits an explicitly conditional reference with
the interruption gap removed, but actual age and injury/surgery evidence retained.
That gives 690.57 conditional PA before suspension. The 142/162 eligible-game
factor gives 605.32 conditional PA; appearance probability gives 605.12 expected
PA. The nearly 100% appearance estimate is NOT a medical recovery probability.
It is also not demonstrably calibrated for this rare recovery profile.

Keeping the ordinary missed-year penalty gives 595.12 conditional PA before
suspension and 520.32 expected PA afterward. These are two model sensitivities,
not a 520–605 confidence interval or a fitted mixture of recovery outcomes.
Neither case subtracts surgery and suspension periods independently. No public
projection or future Tatis outcome supplies an input, probability or PA override.

## The checks that prevent a false success

The broader reference loses on 1,988 eligible unrestricted regular-player
forecasts: PA RMSE is 159.04 versus 154.06 for the stronger employment benchmark,
and mean absolute error is 125.35 versus 123.31. Participation Brier and log loss
also worsen. It predicts 841,997 PA against 821,446 actual, versus the benchmark's
809,086. This is not a suitable general replacement.

The [fifteen player walks](hitter-finite-return-baseline-player-review.md) reveal
why the improved Tatis forecast cannot settle that decision. Devon Travis gets
800 reference PA after 432, with medical-history inputs contributing positively
to a raw 851-PA estimate. The ceiling conceals rather than repairs the absurdity.
Moustakas gets only 119 after a 598-PA/38-HR season because unsigned-player
participation is excessively discounted. These are opposite serious failures.

For Tatis, the actual interruption profile has one training comparison. Removing
the gap supplies 90 distinct ordinary-role comparisons, but adding surgery and
substantial recent medical absence leaves zero. Those 90 are not 90 medically
comparable cleared returnees. Recorded surgery increases his conditional reference
through a positive fitted association; it is not evidence surgery reduces his
future risk. Sparse, uneven medical coverage and role selection qualify that
association. The source-to-forecast repair works; clinical calibration remains open.

The candidate changes only Tatis. All other 30,518 forecasts retain the stronger
benchmark exactly, and hitting is fixed everywhere. Original-cohort PA RMSE
therefore changes 60.2698 to 60.1781 solely through this already-exposed case.
That arithmetic improvement is not independent general validation. Its
batting-plus-replacement error remains slightly worse than the selected anchor
(.435236 versus .435133); fixing his PA does not solve hitting or full player value.
No nominal bootstrap interval would turn one exposed case into fresh confirmation.

The public matched cohort excludes zero-origin-MLB-PA players, so it does not
include Tatis's 2023 forecast. Its PA comparison is unchanged. Do not claim that
this repair closed the Steamer gap. Archive-vintage qualifications remain.

## Decision and the next repair

Retain the finite-budget implementation, dated readiness facts and working
career-role reconstruction as qualified research. Reject this fitted reference
as a general opportunity replacement; do not deploy its 605-PA Tatis scenario
as a validated unconditional forecast. No follow-up penalty/parameter sweep,
named-player exception or health-cleared assumption is authorized by this result.

The next repair within the Tatis case is separating supported ordinary role
estimation from poorly supported clinical associations. Explicitly unknown
medical coverage must not teach a beneficial injury effect, and medical return
expectations need a conditional scenario rather than false recovery certainty.
Retain this failed reference and its player cases; do not repeat the same fit.
Resolve that mechanism before advancing to Hoskins's workload or another component.
