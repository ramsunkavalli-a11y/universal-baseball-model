# Japan and Korea history connected to dated model inputs

2026-10-04. Public international data are available and usable. The completed
Japan and Korea batting collections cover 2005–2024. This milestone connects
reviewed identities, actual professional counts, birth dates and dated MLB roster
context to an additive input ledger. It does not change projections or establish
an accuracy improvement. The practical hitter goal remains unfinished.

## What changed

Official KBO English identity profiles were collected for all 584 IDs with at
least one 100-PA season in the source window. This was a collection priority,
not historical forecast eligibility. Exact normalized name and birth date match
464 unique people in the pinned register: 164 have MLBAM join keys and 300 do
not. Another 76 profiles lack the necessary name or birth date, and 44 have no
exact match. All 6,473 original batting rows remain, including 998 source IDs
outside the collection priority. An MLBAM identifier does not prove MLB arrival.

The additive Japan/Korea inputs cover 641 player-origins and 282 distinct people
in the existing dated population ledger. Of these, 493 were original source
origins and 148 are source additions. These are not 282 approved hitter forecasts:
the original broad source also contains pitchers and unresolved roles. All 83,300
population rows and 30,506 saved forecasts remain unchanged. The overlay contains
three annual component-count windows, observed exposure, verified age when
available and dated roster context. Missing other-league history stays unknown,
not a certified zero. Raw foreign counts are not MLB-equivalent performance.

Independent reconstruction checked all 42,306 component-count fields, all 771
mover-pair role records and 1,110 chronological held-player support groups.
Eleven fixed player input walks and the earlier ordinary/unmapped source controls
remain available. The focused international suite passes 50 tests, and all 31
protected forecast files are unchanged. These checks establish source consistency,
not predictive quality. The separate completed review supersedes the preparation
report's pending-review label without rewriting that report.

## Baseball checks and corrections

Hyeseong Kim before 2025 now has 1,754 KBO PA over the three observed years,
an age of 25.99 at the January information date and a dated hitter/40-man hint.
His old expected MLB PA is still 0.3409; it has not been repaired by changing a
number manually. Before 2018, Ohtani has 732 NPB batting PA in the three-year
window and a two-way hint. His reserve-list absence must not erase the dated
agreement or his professional experience. Neither example guarantees workload.

Two sample-design corrections were required before fitting. First, a normal
2021 MLB season following overseas play in 2020 is usable; it is not the same
problem as the shortened 2020 MLB season or canceled 2020 MiLB season. The
original blanket exclusion remains documented but is superseded by the
[domestic-season amendment](hitter-foreign-2020-support-amendment.md).

Second, Ha-Seong Kim's 2021 domestic count metadata says X, meaning Unknown.
His dated January 2021 roster supplies independent infielder evidence. The
[role amendment](hitter-foreign-role-input-amendment.md) permits a broad hitter
hint only when domestic-side metadata is unknown, using that year's dated
preseason evidence or other positive-PA domestic stints in the same completed
year. It does not overwrite recorded pitchers, certify a defensive position or
use a current overseas position retroactively. Mixed and conflicting evidence
remain explicit; a hint is not proof of an exclusive hitter role.

## What is still insufficient

At the 30-PA pair floor, the complete through-2024 sample contains only 17
distinct qualified consecutive NPB-to-MLB hitter movers and 10 KBO-to-MLB movers.
Actual historical held-player folds have fewer. Hyeseong Kim's 2024-origin fold
has nine qualified direct Korean mover people, not hundreds of independent
comparisons; age/contact/power support can be much thinner. AAA bridge and reverse
moves provide additional evidence but are different selected populations.
Repeated seasons and a large domestic sample do not remove that limitation.

This supports a small, regularized translation rather than a complicated
overseas-specific tree model. It does not support a fixed nationality bonus,
raw league averages as MLB talent, a universal PA assignment, or rejection of
foreign inputs because direct samples are small. Park exposure and historical
overseas roles are not fully qualified. Taiwan, Mexico and winter-league sources
remain leads rather than completed historical collections.

original evaluation identities; compare corrected dated context alone against

additions separately, while retaining non-arrivals. Before fitting, declare the
league/AAA bridge, shrinkage, chronology, player separation, missing-history
fallbacks, outcomes and support limits. Walk both gains and misses through their
actual inputs and intermediate forecasts before choosing a candidate. No new
