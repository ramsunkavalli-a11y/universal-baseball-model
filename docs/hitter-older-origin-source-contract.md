# Older hitter training population audit

2026-10-04. The current practical hitter model starts its training origins in
2011, although cached component histories extend earlier. Audit those earlier
sources before expanding training. This work does not fit a model or change a
forecast. More observations are useful only if their population, histories and
future MLB labels answer the same question as the current model.

## Population and source checks

Inventory cached 2003–2010 hitting components and origin populations. Rebuild
2008–2010 hitter snapshots from all dated roster and season-use evidence, not
from the subset that subsequently reached MLB. Keep inactive roster hitters,
failed prospects and players with prior MLB experience. Distinguish official
debut history from absence in a limited statistics window. Unresolved history
remains unknown; do not automatically label such players never-debuted.

Use repaired current 2008–2010 counts and basic season statistics, with the
original dated October 15 roster captures. These rosters are historical listing
evidence, not certified December 31 ownership or complete medical availability.
Check original and rebuilt memberships and identify any additional players.
Do not use future success to define eligibility, rank, age, peers or exposure.

Three-year predictors for an origin in 2008 require 2006 and 2007. The old bulk
files omit sport 15. Recover only those missing two seasons, with separate
hitting and pitching queries, short-page pagination, duplicate/accounting checks,
historical team-set checks and explicit PA versus BF discrepancies. Save raw
responses and request parameters. Do not overwrite the original sources or
quietly revise an older experiment. Audit earlier rookie coverage and multiple
team/league aggregation rather than assuming absent rows mean zero history.

Verify cached component and snapshot hashes against their original reports.
Reconstruct cached raw counts and dated league/position metadata; reconcile
overlapping 2008–2010 current and old person/sport totals. Check positive MLB
PA outcomes against the completed official 2004–2009 and 2009–2025 inventories,
including their overlapping 2009 counts. No protected 2026 outcomes may be read.
Observed inactive MLB years have zero participation, not zero hitting ability.
An outcome inventory does not establish the prospect denominator by itself.

## Review and allowed conclusions

Fixed source cases are Trout 2009, Harper 2010, Hosmer 2008, Moustakas 2008,
Montero 2008, Belt 2010 and Pujols 2008 if eligible. Do not manufacture a row if
one is absent. Add an ordinary inactive case selected by sorted player identity.
For each show actual season/level counts, recorded age and position, three-year
history coverage, debut evidence, actual next-year MLB PA and four peers selected
without outcomes by origin, dominant level, debut state and age/exposure.
There are no candidate gains or harms because no forecast is fitted here.

Save source integrity, population coverage, remaining unsupported predictors
and player-review completion separately. Report how many potential training
rows and active MLB labels exist, including non-arrivals. Do not treat a source
pass as predictive validation or claim all old-era input families are available.
Preseason ranking, rights, draft, status and learned level adjustments need
their own compatible cutoff provenance before the matched model comparison.

After the source review, the next permitted modeling step is a matched
restricted-versus-extended-history comparison with identical feature definitions
and the existing evaluation identities. Preserve the current full-history and
translated/ranking anchors. Actual held-player/time membership checks and
profile support must precede every fit. Unknown early history cannot be filled
with zero to make an earlier origin fit the current schema. No promotion,
explorer replacement or frozen forecast change is authorized by this audit.
