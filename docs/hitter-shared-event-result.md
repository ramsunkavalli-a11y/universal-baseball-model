# Shared hitter event learning does not replace the current model

2026-10-04. Player walkthrough complete for 45 cases. The fixed shared event
comparison is complete: seventy fitted heads replay, compatible labels reconstruct
from dated counts, original membership/PA/established talent remain unchanged,
and the two arms do not improve both primary historical losses. Retain the
current model. This is exposed development evidence, not protected confirmation.

## Exact result

All 30,506 original forecasts remain, including non-arrivals and exits. Future
targets end in 2025. The [contract](hitter-shared-event-contract.md) isolates
talent for hitters without prior MLB experience and keeps existing MLB branches.
The thirteen additions are separately scored. The [source review](hitter-shared-event-source-review.md)
preceded every fit; the [player review](hitter-shared-event-player-review.md)
explains actual gains, harms, false highs/lows and misleading cancellations.

incumbent forecast for these rows. The fourteen original foreign never-debut
forecasts improve hitting modestly but expect only 24.6 PA against 410 actual.

The 2627 matched public rows have prior MLB evidence. Both new arms therefore
retain the incumbent exactly, by construction. There is no new Steamer/ZiPS
win or PA improvement. Existing public vintage, conversion and park/environment
qualifications remain; the meaningful workload gap is not closed by this test.

## Disposition and next boundary

Integrity and required walkthroughs pass; profile support is qualified and
baseball results are mixed. Predictive improvement fails. Do not promote either
arm, change a frozen forecast or reject foreign/minor production generally.
The second scalar regression is not the defined conversion of a future event
distribution into runs. Resolve that endpoint distinction with a fixed, bounded
component/value check before any more model-family or penalty experiments.
Do not bypass small-sample/transport uncertainty by treating these probabilities
as known true talent. Keep age/pedigree and the incumbent's useful MLB branches
visible. No team-record or college-source work reopens.

The broader goal remains active: candidate selection, new immutable forecast
freeze, research explorer and the authorized once-only 2026 evaluation remain.
2026 outcomes are still unopened and the original protected package verifies.
Next-year batting evidence does not establish full WAR, control-year or trade value.
