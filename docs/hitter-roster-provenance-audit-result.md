# Historical roster conflicts do not justify a blanket correction

2026-10-03. The source review is complete, with nine actual player walkthroughs
and 27 independently replayed current-model heads. No forecast changed. The
returned 40Man listing remains useful but qualified context, not a certified
historical reserve-rights database. Replacing it with the last explicit roster
event would create its own errors; flipping whichever flag improves a player's
realized forecast would be outcome-driven data editing.

## What was checked across the population

All 480 saved team/date captures reconcile with 18,708 unique memberships across
2009–2024. This includes the separately recovered 2020 source used in later
training, not an invented MiLB season. Seven duplicate entries have matching
identities and count once each. Every one of the 63,282 current-source listing
inputs agrees with the combined raw returned sets. All 30,506 evaluation
identities and forecasts are preserved.

Thus the current flag is faithfully extracted from these responses. That does
not prove the server returned complete December reserve membership. The
[locked review](hitter-roster-provenance-audit-contract.md) compares those sets
with captured 2015–2024 transactions, keeping older coverage unknown and
later effective/resolution dates separate. It recognizes narrow explicit
selections and 60-day activations, outright/release/designated-assignment/free
agency events. Generic signings, transfers and assignments remain ambiguous.
Every later captured transaction is retained in each case timeline.

The diagnostic finds 1,612 source-row conflicts, including 1,165 evaluation
forecasts. These are **review flags, not 1,165 established source errors**.

People can appear in multiple years and groups; do not add the distinct-person
columns as an independent sample size. Old selections do not certify continuous
membership, and an old free-agency event may be superseded by a signing the
narrow classifier deliberately treats as ambiguous. Subsequent 60-day transfers
can also temporarily remove reserve membership. This diagnostic is not an
ordered reconstruction of every roster transition.

That distinction matters: the model's soft listing input is not the same as
legal eligibility, health, a guaranteed deal or a protected prospect's readiness.
Requested dates and internally consistent extraction do not certify those
meanings. Returned current status and parent-team fields are not used to infer
historical health or rights. Historical publication vintages remain qualified.

## How the player checks work

Eight cases were fixed before the diagnostic. Carlos Beltrán was added as the
earliest origin and lowest row ID in the listed-negative class, without choosing
on his future performance. Every case has actual three-year counts, cutoff raw
transactions, the requested roster row or its absence, all opportunity inputs,
saved opportunity and batting paths, training support and four origin-selected
peers. Improvement and deterioration categories for a new model are empty:
there is no new model or replacement forecast.

The listing toggle below changes that one input in the already fitted
opportunity heads. It holds batting fixed, applies the original active-PA bounds
and definitive-eligibility rules, and changes no saved forecast. It explains
mechanics only: artificial combinations may be unsupported; the toggle is not
causal, validated or scored as a competing system. The actual next-year outcome
does not choose which membership flag is correct.

All PA refer to the following calendar year's MLB play. Batting rates are custom
batting wins above average per 600 PA. Delivered values are the compatible
batting-plus-replacement proxy, not published WAR, six years of control or trade
value. Generic peers match origin, stage, prior debut, age and three-year MLB
workload; they are not equivalent injury, foreign-talent or roster-rights controls.

403 MLB PA/16 HR, 180 AAA PA/12 HR and 452 AA PA/17 HR; current MLB PA is zero.

The simple positive-event flag comes from the **2023 selection**, not a new
December 2024 certification. It does not reconstruct the intervening 60-day
transfer and unqualified activation. Contemporaneous
[MLB reporting](https://www.mlb.com/reds/news/reds-offseason-questions-2024-2025)
expected McLain to take second base. That supports known intended use, not a
certified year-end membership snapshot or complete recovery.

Current appearance 44.67% times 294.75 active PA gives 131.65 expected PA against
577 actual. The saved listing toggle gives 295.87, still far short. Batting is
+.251 per 600, with .466 expected value against .706 actual. Marcano, Mauricio,
Franco and Apostel later get zero, 184, zero and zero PA for different reasons.
Their outcomes and large broad training counts do not establish shoulder-return
support. Source inconsistency is real review work; an automatic flag flip or
universal boost is not yet a validated solution.

## Disposition and next step

Source extraction and 27 saved-head replays pass. Four focused tests check
future-date exclusion, conflicting effective dates, ambiguous assignments and
same-date opposite events. These establish execution, not accurate reserve rights
or a predictive gain. Cases remain qualified, with generic peers and imperfect
historical capture. The unchanged current model still misses important readiness,
return and hitting outcomes and the practical public MAE allowance.

Keep the returned listing as a clearly labeled historical proxy. Do not replace
it with the last explicit event or flip all conflicts. A later inferred reserve
state would require an ordered timeline, intervening 60-day removal/restoration,
later signings and claims, unknown contract class, and missing older coverage,
followed by a locked fair model comparison. This diagnostic supplies neither
that reconstruction nor its validation. Close the diagnostic without another
flag or parameter sweep.

Next carry the actual evidence and source ambiguity into the research handoff:
show returned-list presence as a proxy, flag conflicting or unresolved availability
and foreign history, and retain the original candidate forecasts. Do not expose
the synthetic probes as selectable improved models. Useful uncertainty and
supported prospect readiness remain substantive model work, not solved by this
source review. The full hitter goal remains active; frozen 2026 and production
deployment are unchanged.
