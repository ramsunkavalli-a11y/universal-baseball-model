# The hitting estimate is close to public systems on a common event measure

2026-10-03. Eleven actual reviews complete. This no-fit audit replaces neither
forecasts nor old results. It corrects the comparison units and expands the
public check beyond recently debuted players.

The old public score centered projected events on the origin league but centered
actual events on the target league. The [locked audit](practical-hitter-public-units-v51-contract.md)
instead compares the same fixed-weight event numerator per total PA. UBM's
implied numerator comes from its unchanged relative-rate forecast plus the
origin environment. No future league mean is inserted into a forecast.
For readable units all systems and actual results are then centered on the same
origin. This is not official wOBA, park-neutral true talent, full WAR or trade value.

Conditional rates score only observed future MLB PA: 2,088 of the 2,627 broader
rows, including 634 older-player rows. Workload and delivered offense retain
all matched non-arrivals. Plain ZiPS PA represent conditional exposure, not
a workload forecast. Missing public forecasts remain in the separate coverage
ledger; these public matches do not certify every minor leaguer.

The main conclusion is proximity, not superiority. Nominal broader rate MSE
difference versus Steamer is -0.1033, player-clustered interval -0.1913 to
-0.0048; versus ZiPS -0.0284, interval -0.1230 to +0.0825. These are repeatedly
exposed development results with unknown exact public archive dates, differing
park treatment and a custom fixed-event metric. UBM loses target 2023 to both
public systems. Do not market a pooled point score as an established system win.

## The remaining playing time gap is more specific

Across broader matches UBM predicts 652,656 PA versus 660,776 actual; Steamer
predicts 695,055. Sensible totals still hide player mistakes. The same original
1,789 matches retain the prior PA scores and fail the declared 15% MAE goal;
the broader group's MAE is 16.0% above Steamer, still a miss.

A source-information diagnostic finds 386 public forecasts at one PA. Of those,
302 have zero actual MLB PA. UBM predicts 22,283 PA versus 5,354 actual and
Steamer 386; within that group MAE is 52.58 versus 11.12. On the other 2,241
rows MAE is 115.50 versus 106.23, an 8.7% gap. These diagnostic groups were
formed after scoring; no rows are removed from the headline or used as model
inputs. Some public archives plainly express near-zero availability, but their
exact dates are unknown. Later injury/roster information is a plausible source
of advantage, not proven for every one-PA forecast. A one-PA prediction is also
not automatically right: 84 such players do appear.

## What the players show

The [full walkthrough](../reports/model-evidence/practical-hitter-public-units-v51/player-walkthrough.md)
preserves actual source stats, public projected events, unit calculations and
origin-selected peers. Judge's 2024-origin UBM and Steamer hitting indexes are
nearly identical (.39587 versus .39643), but UBM predicts 531 PA versus Steamer
625 and actual 679. Winn is also less pessimistic on talent than both public
systems, yet gets too little playing time. Steer remains a real weakness in
both translated hitting and opportunity.

Guerrero's regression after his 48-HR season helps relative to Steamer; Judge's
regression before his 62-HR season hurts. Acuna is a false low before a huge
season and a false high before a reduced one. Neither direction is evidence
that every star should receive a universal boost or penalty. Jimenez's almost
exact contribution hides opposing rate/PA errors. Alfaro's zero contribution
can be accidentally close despite too much expected PA. Unsuccessful peers
remain visible; future absences do not supply origin injury inputs.

## Decision

Existing hitting ability plus reviewed binary readiness is the clearest
development candidate; the new V50 skill-shrinkage forecasts do not replace
the stronger batting estimate. Finish its practical historical handoff and
benchmark explorer, keeping fast-entry prospects, elite-power compression,
availability and untranslated/contextual talent as explicit remaining gaps.
The model is useful research, not yet a certified full hitter/value system.
Protected 2026 and the frozen/deployed forecast remain unchanged; goal active.
