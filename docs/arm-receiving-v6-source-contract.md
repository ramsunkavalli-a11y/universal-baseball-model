# Check outfield arm and first base receiving opportunities

2026-10-06, before another model comparison. Use the existing native position
ledger as an independent run anchor, but recover actual opportunity denominators
before calling a run rate defensive skill. Do not count assists alone or treat
first-base innings as receiving opportunities.

Inspect completed 2022 and 2025 public MLB arm-value and first-base receiving
pages, with explicit years, regular-season individual identities and permissive
one-opportunity eligibility. Preserve raw responses and observed parameters.
Never query 2026 outcomes. Parse JSON only; do not execute page scripts.

The [arm-value description](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/baserunning?type=Fld)
compares outcomes with estimated advancement opportunities and includes holds
as well as advances and outs. Arm value is not simply measured throwing speed.
The [first-base receiving page](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/first-base-scoops-receiving?type=fielder_3&season%5B%5D=2025&splitYear=1&min=1&minSplit=1&gameType%5B%5D=R)
offers receiving outcomes and opportunities grouped by throw characteristics.
Confirm its actual metadata, precision and native-run conversion before use.
Missing/older measurements remain unknown, not zero-quality observations.

Reconcile recorded opportunities, components, source years and identities.
Compare arm totals across LF/CF/RF with the native ledger while preserving any
other-position arm credit; do not silently force that credit into outfield.
Compare receiving with the 1B native component, separate from ground-ball range.
Do not combine incomparable position/opportunity definitions to force agreement.

Walk fixed Judge, Betts, Happ, Castellanos and Kiermaier for arms, and Freeman,
Olson, Walker, Alonso and Hoskins for receiving when source-eligible. Add the
two smallest positive samples and three exposure/ID-selected peers per case.
Show raw counts, adjustments, unrounded numerator, rate and source gaps.

Only after the source/player checkpoint may that exact method extend to arm
2016–2025 and receiving 2021–2025, subject to actual historical availability.
Then count complete future MLB quality windows and genuine chronological support
before a separate neutral-versus-history comparison. An unavailable source or
short receiving chronology is an explicit limitation, not permission to relabel
next-year innings-based contribution as isolated talent. Frozen forecasts,
explorer and completed 2026 evaluation remain unchanged.
