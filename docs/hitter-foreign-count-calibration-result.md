# Overseas count calibration: no replacement approved

2026-10-05. The new count-likelihood calibration is executed and reviewed, but
does not establish a useful whole-model improvement. Keep current forecasts.
The [player review](hitter-foreign-count-calibration-player-review.md) explains
the gains, failures and misleading error cancellations. The [contract](hitter-foreign-count-calibration-contract.md)
was locked before any fit. The earlier failed direct-conversion experiment was
not rerun unchanged: this changed the calibration response loss AND prior/data
scale, while preserving its historical sources and held calibration people.

## Exact scope and baseline

All 30,506 original historical forecasts plus thirteen previously missing
qualified additions remain. Origins are 2016–18 and 2021–24; targets finish
by 2025. No new 2026 outcome read, model, forecast or explorer revision occurred.
New 2025 overseas source data are preserved but not used to revise the final
2026 test. The completed 2026 evaluation remains final.

The new source-only talent applies when an observed NPB/KBO season within three
years has at least thirty PA and is later than every substantial domestic season.
Same-year ambiguous work and newer MLB work keep the anchor. All participation
probabilities, conditional PA and expected PA are unchanged. There are 126
qualifying forecasts: 114 original and twelve additions, all translation-supported.
Only five original routed forecasts have target MLB PA, versus six additions.
The broader 253-original foreign-source diagnostic contains stale overseas
histories after MLB play; it is NOT the applied forecast route.

Original baselines are unchanged current UBM. The thirteen additions have no
current UBM forecast: their fixed comparator is an earlier dated-context domestic
anchor. A newer shared opportunity candidate exists but is not substituted into
this locked component contrast. Thus these totals diagnose this test's fixed
opportunity, not every prior opportunity candidate or the selected 2026 package.
In particular, 145 predicted versus 1,625 actual addition PA must not be presented
as a new best-model workload result.

## Is the new loss better than the saved overseas translation?

Not consistently. On the same original fresh route the saved borrowed
translation has contribution RMSE 0.360870—slightly better than count's 0.361503.
Its equal-origin hitting RMSE is worse (1.977005 versus 1.742057), but that score
gives Dixon's fourteen-PA season a quarter of active-origin weight. An explicitly
post-score, descriptive pooled-PA check reverses that comparison: 0.540841 old
versus 0.574084 new. Additions similarly reverse under pooled PA: 2.132621 old
versus 2.206654 new, despite count's better locked equal-origin score.
Neither sensitivity is used to tune or replace the primary scoring rule.

Fresh original categorical log loss worsens 1.519667 to 1.519910; Brier improves
0.732617 to 0.732240. Weighted HR frequency drops 3.080% to 2.773% versus 1.683%
actual; K rises 24.639% to 25.621% versus 30.056% actual. For six active additions,
log loss worsens 1.438618 to 1.441092 and Brier 0.682899 to 0.683128; K falls
17.975% to 17.413% versus 20.228% actual. Their HR estimate rises 3.101% to
3.239% versus 2.925% actual, although HR RMSE improves. No blanket claim of
restored HR calibration or proper-probability improvement survives these checks.

The old broad 253-source component ledger also worsens categorical log loss
(1.507046 to 1.510243) and Brier (0.710560 to 0.710738). Its twenty active people
include later MLB seasons with stale overseas evidence. That ledger is useful
for diagnosis but cannot establish the fresh route's effective sample size.

All 2,627 matched public original forecasts remain; NONE uses this route.
Public scores are exactly unchanged. No benchmark gain over Steamer/ZiPS follows
from this experiment. Snapshot, park and environment comparability limits remain.

Thames and Suzuki show meaningful foreign information, but Ohtani/Hyeseong worsen,

wrongly displace substantial MLB history. Selected mover support is sparse;
parks/opponents, young two-way development and proper sample reliability are
not resolved. Count-likelihood theory is not baseball transportability.

and their dated employment evidence before proposing any new fit. Identify which
