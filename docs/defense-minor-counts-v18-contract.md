# Audit minor fielding evidence against later MLB ability

2026-10-07. This source and support audit follows the completed position-model
review. It produces no fitted prediction, accuracy winner or forecast change.
The question is whether saved minor fielding counts support a defensible test
of later MLB ability, with lower levels included rather than hidden in an
all-level survivor correlation.

## Sources and measurements

Recover the actual count fields from the hashed raw captures underlying
`defense-position-opportunity-v7`: minor seasons 2009–2019, the disjoint 2019
advanced-rookie repair, and 2021–2024. Replay each source identity and innings
against the accepted usage rows. Do not refetch, merge MLB counts into minor
rates, use attached clubs as certified team exposure, or invent a 2020 minor
season. The early sport-16 scope is combined rookie baseball; an attached league
does not certify that its counts are exclusively DSL or complex. Preserve that
qualification and the eliminated short-season levels.

Inventory putouts, assists, errors, chances, throwing errors, double plays,
caught stealing, stolen bases allowed, passed balls, wild pitches, catcher
interference and pickoffs. Pickoffs are credited outs, not pickoff throws or a
direct measure of confidence in the catcher's arm.
Nonexistent/null/invalid fields remain unknown; explicit zeros remain recorded
counts, not proof of measured average talent. Record chances identity and
throwing-error inconsistencies separately. Any derived rate requires all of
its count fields and positive exposure. Catcher steal success is conditional
on attempts and battery context; passed balls per inning are not difficulty-
adjusted blocking skill. First-base putouts and catcher putouts are strongly
driven by team opportunities. This audit does not award run values to these stats.

## Origin population and future labels

Include each player and defensive position C through RF with at least 25
minor defensive outs in the current observed origin season. Keep every level
and its counts, including promoted players. Origins are 2009–2019 and 2021–2024;
2020 has no current minor source and is not silently manufactured. Age uses
immutable birth date or the existing dated origin panel; if neither is available
it stays unknown. Do not substitute a present-day hydrated age. Describe current
level by the largest origin position exposure, with a deterministic tie break;
retain all other levels and their exposure separately. Flag any prior/current
MLB fielding from the existing full official ledger without calling that proof
of a batting debut. Missing input years and source boundary remain explicit.

Inventory fixed three- and five-calendar-year windows after each origin ending
by 2025. Keep incomplete windows in coverage without scored quality. Range at
1B through RF uses the independently qualified position-specific native ledger,
not aggregate runs distributed across innings. A usable label requires at least
1,500 pooled native outs, two measured seasons and no missing measurement at
positive official exposure for that position within the complete window.
Pre-2016 positive official exposure is unmeasured, not zero range. MLB's short
2020 season enters at its actual exposure; it cannot stand for a minor season.
Future fielding at other positions is recorded separately, not confirmation
of origin-position quality. Units are native runs per 500 defensive innings.

At C, inventory separately the previously certified difficulty-adjusted native
throwing and blocking records. Throwing requires 100 tracked attempts and two
measured seasons; blocking requires 3,000 chances and two measured seasons.
Missing/invalid measurement with positive official catcher exposure makes the
quality unknown. Use the existing exact run numerators and opportunity definitions,
not rounded display values. Units are native runs per 100 attempts or 1,000
blocking chances. No lower-minor framing test is implied by these count fields.

Non-arrivals, position changes, short cameos and measurement gaps remain in the
origin ledger. They have unknown MLB quality, not zero talent. Record which
origin profiles become measurable; conditional survivor quality cannot certify
the entire prospect population. Later developed ability is not exact talent
at the original age. Record elapsed time and actual future opportunity paths.
