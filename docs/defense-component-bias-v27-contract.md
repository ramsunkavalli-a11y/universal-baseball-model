# First base and catcher forecast bias diagnosis

The existing defensive forecasts add too much value in the matched 2023 to
2025 cohorts. This audit separates historical skill estimates, forecast
opportunities and incomplete measurement before choosing a repair. It does not
fit a model, change a prediction, or test a replacement prior.

## Population and arithmetic

Retain every saved player and origin in the reviewed defense value comparison:
origins 2022, 2023 and 2024, predicting 2023, 2024 and 2025. Examine first-base
range and framing as the focal channels; report all twelve channels as context.
Use the saved own-repertoire opportunity forecast and historical skill estimate.
Keep unknown positive-exposure results missing. Certified absence of defensive
exposure gives zero delivered runs, not zero talent.

For every measured row with an available native opportunity denominator,
reproduce the saved actual-opportunity diagnostic:

predicted runs = forecast opportunities times historical rate divided by its unit

actual-opportunity prediction = actual opportunities times that same rate
divided by its unit

forecast error = opportunity contribution plus rate contribution, where the
opportunity contribution is predicted minus actual-opportunity prediction, and
the rate contribution is actual-opportunity prediction minus observed runs.

This identity is an explanatory decomposition, not a causal effect or a usable
forecast. Summed errors can cancel; also show absolute error and squared error.
Show full channel-measured cohorts, the unchanged complete twelve-channel subset,
actual defenders and measured-history versus unknown-history groups. No changing
membership to make a comparison look better.

## References and source checks

Reconstruct history from qualified native annual records through each origin,
with recency weights 1, 0.5 and 0.25. First-base range uses 3000 prior outs and
runs per 1500 outs; framing uses 6000 prior pitches and runs per 1000 pitches.
Verify that the denominator and run definition agree with each stored source.
Compare origin-season full qualified totals and rates with held-player-fold
references excluding the entire player fold. These are descriptive reference
checks, not automatically new priors. Do not force first base or framing to
zero, assume all infield positions share a reference, or impose a future quota.
Report observed future totals only as outcomes, never forecast inputs.

## Players and comparisons

Fixed origin-2022 cases are Freddie Freeman, Matt Olson, Paul Goldschmidt,
Vladimir Guerrero Jr., J.T. Realmuto, Willson Contreras, Sean Murphy and Austin
Hedges. Each receives three comparisons selected using origin, stage, dominant
defensive role, age and historical exposure, in that order, without future
outcomes. Add the largest positive and negative focal-channel error and the
ordinary measured defender nearest the median absolute error in each channel.
These additional cases explain exposed errors and are not fresh validation.

Trace annual source records, weighted counts and runs, shrinkage, rate,
forecast and actual opportunities, actual-opportunity runs and observed runs.
Preserve unknown outcomes and inspect other applicable channels in catcher cases.
If the decomposition reveals a specific defect, distinguish it from a plausible
miss, measurement noise, selected survivor population or role change.

## Completion and limits

Recompute the decomposition independently from the frozen input columns and
reconstruct every reviewed historical rate from its annual sources. Verify
source hashes and frozen forecast, protected-evaluation and explorer bytes.
Persist compressed machine evidence and a readable player walkthrough before
updating the controlling plan. No 2026 outcome access, selection or UI change.

A diagnosed annual bias does not establish future MLB talent prediction,
minor-league transport or a replacement forecasting method. Choose one next
mechanism supported by this diagnosis; if the rate is not the cause, do not
solve an opportunity problem with a new skill learner. The broader defense goal
remains active.
