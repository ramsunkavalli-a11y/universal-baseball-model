# Minor fielding counts help a few supported players but leave most talent unresolved

2026-10-07. The repaired count recipe makes sensible small improvements for
Volpe, Rafaela and Meadows without changing the existing age, position and level
baseline. It also prevents an unsupported minus-13-run grade from Frick's tiny
third-base stint. These are useful repairs, but not a broadly validated minor
defense model: only three measured primary players receive the correction.
Keep the benchmark and research inputs; do not promote this augmentation or
retune its guards to make more players pass.

## What was compared

The fixed comparison retains all 13,133 eligible 2021/2022 minor-origin positions
with no prior/current MLB fielding. Quality is native range runs per 500 innings
over the next three calendar years at the origin position. A measurable target
needs 1,500 outs, two measured seasons and no missing positive official exposure.
Missing quality, nonarrival and position switches are not zero ability.

Three arms use identical identities and labels: the old selected count model,
the exact saved age/position/level baseline, and that baseline plus a four-signal
count correction. The new correction uses favorable nonthrowing-error and
throwing-error deviations, IF assists and OF putouts, with reviewed sample
regression. It has no intercept and cannot refit the benchmark's age or position
coefficients. No park, pitcher traffic or play-difficulty correction is present.
First-base putouts are not receiving evidence.

Training excludes held people at all origins and requires entire future label
windows to be mature. Each training residual uses a held-player nuisance
prediction whose penalty selection also excludes those people. Ninety nested
cells are recorded; 30 have sufficient fitting support. Ten of 40 nuisance
choices have no supported measured validation and use the declared penalty 100.
Twenty choose 10 and twenty choose 100. The count penalty stays fixed at 100.

The original quality execution stopped on a tuple/list identity comparison,
after nested tuning but before nuisance or correction fitting. All 40 audits
matched exactly after JSON normalization. A sibling runner resumed from saved
nested fits; the originals and both amendments remain preserved. No retuning,
membership change or 2026 outcome access followed the interruption.

Primary MAE improves 2.21093 to 2.19462. Stress MAE slightly worsens 2.36234 to
2.36363. The comparison does not pass the declared improvement screen because
the primary RMSE interval does not exclude zero. All other declared aggregate
harm checks pass. These are exposed development comparisons, not protected tests.

Only 35 of 6,547 primary forecasts receive a correction, including three with
measured quality; the remaining 6,512 use the exact benchmark. At the stress
origin, 13 of 6,586 receive one, including six measured players. Guards require
ten distinct position/level training people, five in the age/exposure joint
profile, and all signals inside the position/level training rectangle. These
conservative guards avoid extrapolation but do not prove the surviving profiles
are sufficient. Three primary gains cannot certify a lower-minor population.

The primary zero interval endpoint has a concrete explanation: all three changed
measured people improve, while the others are unchanged. A 62-person bootstrap
has about a 4.6 percent chance of drawing none of those three people, producing
exactly zero difference. The result is not a rounding artifact or a broad win.

Origin-known peers do not uniformly succeed. Young's Flores/Cerny/Allen peers lack
measured later CF quality. Rafaela's Barrosa/Mears/Doston and Meadows's
Frelick/Canario/Rosario also lack qualifying CF quality; some have short positive
MLB cameos, which stay visible without becoming certified grades. Volpe's Rocchio
peer is measured and close to neutral; Mauricio/Tena remain unmeasured. Greene's
Ramos peer improves directionally, +0.351 to -0.268 versus -6.362 later, while
Thomas is positive and unchanged. His Rodríguez peer is positive but too thin
at the source to receive a correction. First-base peers include unmeasured
Martin/Yerzy and measured Montero at -2.675. Outcomes are not forced into a
successful-prospect narrative.

The fallback protects Frick from an unsupported extreme, but does not solve
Young, Crow-Armstrong or Edwards. Raw errors do not measure range difficulty;
putout and assist frequencies depend on pitchers, batted-ball traffic, parks,
scoring and deployment. Pooling three future calendar years also mixes talent
development with measurement noise. Same-position grades cannot certify later
performance at another position. None of these limitations is repaired by a
small pooled gain or by predicting almost no future playing time.

## Decision and next work

Retain the reviewed count-calibration recipe and real fallback mechanism as
research tools. Do not promote this sparse augmentation, retune its thresholds,
or reject all minor defensive evidence. The useful MLB-history components stay
the practical skill baseline. Delivered runs, full WAR and control/trade value
were not improved or tested by this conditional-quality comparison.

Next audit the missing double-play channel and the possibility of older,
comparable MLB fielding measurements. Establish actual numerator, opportunity,
position scope and measurement coverage before proposing a learner. This targets
two remaining gaps: missing defensive skills and too few mature prospect
examples. Preserve the existing framing, throwing, blocking, arm, receiving and
position/value evidence instead of repeating their completed weight sweeps.
Only a supported quality signal proceeds to a matched delivered-value check.

All source priors, nested choices, nuisance fits, corrections, 13,133 predictions,
group scores and person bootstraps replay independently. The archive preserves
exact execution bytes. To recover omitted local duplicates use
`scripts/minor_range_archive_access_v21.py --restore`; this restores artifacts,
not new model fits. The 7,519 local prior files were removed only after matching
each to the verified archive. Raw sources, old models and frozen forecasts remain.

The player review is complete with the above limitations. The full defense goal
remains active. Frozen forecasts, the explorer, Lovich's separate batting repair
and 2026 model selection are unchanged.
