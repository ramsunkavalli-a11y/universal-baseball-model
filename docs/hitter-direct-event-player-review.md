## Judgment

The defined conversion obeys sensible event-value directions, unlike unconstrained
conditional regression coefficients. It still fails as a deployed replacement.
Good contact probability scores can coexist with biased power and poor value
forecasts. Transporting a conditional MLB-returner persistence fit and a selected
same-season level graph to all minor hitters omits development/scouting and does
not guarantee arithmetic event calibration. Retain these component diagnostics;
do not promote direct conversion or tune its constants to the named players.
