# Historical contact ownership repaired before the next projection test

2026-10-04. The eight-season source rebuild is complete. It corrects who receives
credit for a contact while preserving the archived location, trajectory and
result. This is a necessary data repair, not evidence of improved projections.
Keep the current model and frozen forecast unchanged.

## What was checked

The rebuild covers every existing terminal partition in 2016–19 and 2021–24.
There are 4,591,561 retained physical contacts; 4,378,389 have enough information
for the ninety contact-result cells. Conflicting or uncertain source keys remain
quarantined. Bunts, foul-air exclusions and incomplete classification remain
visible outside those cells. No missing year is invented.

Independent player-game counts selected 11,692 exception games before official
inspection. Another 2,520 games were selected without exceptions, twenty per
actual league and season. All 14,212 selected games have matchup authority for
every retained contact sequence. Official evidence corrects 12,100 contact
identities; no unflagged audit game has an identity mismatch. That sample checks
the screening method, not every unflagged game or all scheduled-game coverage.

Current official captures can incorporate historical corrections. They adjudicate
participant ownership here; they are not certified original game-day vintages.
They do not replace the archived geometry or result with modern measurements.
Player-count residuals after correction remain coverage/definition evidence,
not a reason to synthesize contacts. The final receipt saves them by season.

## What this fixes and what it does not

Use the corrected, actual-league raw measurements for a chronology-safe model
test. Keep Mexican League and individual rookie identities; do not merge them
into domestic AAA or call these measurements park-neutral. Old learned park
residuals are not silently imported.

The current hitting/opportunity candidate uses count history rather than this
contact block, so its forecasts do not change. Reynolds gains only one contact;
Kurtz and Langford gain none. Their major readiness/workload misses remain.
Meadows' old gain and McNeil's old harm are also not explained by this source
repair alone. The next experiment must measure actual future MLB hitting and
delivered offense on matched people and references, not claim success from
source correctness or same-level contact persistence.

The first 2016 review's unsigned subtraction error and original output are
preserved separately. Signed differences and their regression test are used
here. Thirty-four focused tests pass. The independent freeze verifier confirms
all 31 locked files and 3,907 players unchanged without opening 2026 outcomes.
Source approval is not model approval or deployment approval; the full practical
hitter goal, including public workload and cohort gaps, remains active.
