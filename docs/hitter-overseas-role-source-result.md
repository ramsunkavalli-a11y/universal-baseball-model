# Employment evidence corrected; role coverage is still limited

2026-10-05. Source-only review, not an accuracy experiment. Both frozen forecast
packages and the completed one-time 2026 evaluation remain unchanged. No new
fit, PA forecast, eligibility change, or explorer update.

## What changed

The old signing parser tested whether a description contained `signed `.
That also matches `assigned ` and `reassigned `. Assignments and status changes
were therefore sometimes recorded as unspecified agreements. The new version
keeps those records as assignment context, separately from actual signing,
activation, release and reserve evidence. An assignment is not proof of no job;
an old agreement is not certification of current rights either.

Across 83,300 historical origins for 22,519 people, employment paths change in
23,172 origins for 6,657 people. Nine numeric employment indicators change in
6,941 origins for 3,244 people. Repeated years are not independent observations.
Clinical evidence, availability restrictions, source membership and outcomes
are untouched. This is a correction to future research inputs, not a claim that
all previously deployed models used this defective feature or now improve.

The completed rebuild stopped before its first job-input walk because the
retained job-feature dictionary omitted origin metadata. A separately sealed
recovery added origin year and prior MLB PA from the same retained forecast row,
reproduced the old inputs, and finished the walks without repeating the rebuild.
The original executable, stop and source outputs are preserved.

## Contract and intended role are different

Ten official reports were reviewed; eight origins have eligible reports and only
four have explicit intended-role information. This handpicked diagnostic pilot
is not population-complete historical training data. Publication vintage is not
independently certified by retrieving an old page today. Same-day reports retain
precision warnings. Three foreign origins have no captured employment record;
that is unknown employment, not no job.

The [Suzuki signing release](https://www.mlb.com/press-release/press-release-cubs-agree-to-terms-with-outfielder-seiya-suzuki-on-a-five-year-ma)
supports a five-year major contract. His
[role report](https://www.mlb.com/news/seiya-suzuki-signs-5-year-deal-with-cubs)
is after the March 18 cutoff and is excluded.
The [Young report](https://www.mlb.com/mets/news/jared-young-signs-contract-with-mets)
describes camp competition despite a major contract. The
[Ohtani report](https://www.mlb.com/news/shohei-ohtani-agrees-to-deal-with-angels-c263134146)
describes rotation and part-time DH expectations. Neither contract labels nor
foreign PA alone encode those distinct situations.

## Disposition and one next step

Retain the versioned parser correction after source checks; no model promotion.
Do not feed the four handpicked role observations into a model or tune to these
names. The next bounded development comparison should change only corrected
employment indicators in the existing opportunity branch, keep talent inputs
and evaluation membership fixed, retain original anchors, and score MLB PA,
probability, delivered batting contribution, totals and named players. Audit
actual chronological/player-separated training support before fitting. Preserve
exits, unknowns and both freezes. This tests a real source correction rather
than repeating the already rejected broad foreign-performance experiment.

Final machine checks are recorded in
`reports/model-evidence/hitter-employment-v3/report.json`; private source receipts,
59 retained walks and two ordinary source controls are under
`reports/generated/hitter-employment-v3`. Completion means reviewed source
evidence, not improved forecasts or a finished player-value model.
