# V33: pooled evidence, existing draft pedigree and safe linear talent

Declared 2026-10-02 after the completed V32 assembly review. This is one bounded
structural batch, not another library tournament or player-specific bonus sweep.

## Question and fixed population

Can a simpler reliability-aware representation improve next-calendar-year MLB
PA and delivered batting-plus-replacement contribution? Retain all V31 training
cells and all 30,506 evaluation identities, including non-arrivals, long careers
and exits. Preserve V24, older N and the identical qualified public comparisons.
No 2026 outcomes, new college collection or deployment. Same mature chronological
targets and whole-player five-fold exclusion as V31. Audit all actual folds and
conditional-active training profiles before any fit. Sparse cases stay scored.

## Source and representation

Rebuild from V31's independently reconciled counts and corrected source panel.
For each of its fourteen distinct league buckets, pool the last three calendar
years' event counts with weights 1, .8, .6 (the familiar 5:4:3 ratio). Use actual
opportunities, not the average of three separately shrunk season rates. Add a
single fixed 100-opportunity prior per pooled event. Retain exposures, current
and lag workloads, age, known debut, position/missingness and captured listing
context. Pool MLB batting value above its own season's replacement allocation
with the same weights and a single 1,200-PA prior; 2020 MLB exposure is actual
60-game evidence, not fabricated full-season observations. Canceled MiLB 2020
adds no observations or zero-performance penalty; retain cancellation flags.
Absent-player histories are not erased because the current year is empty.
These are raw league-specific counts, not park-neutral rates or certified MLEs.

Use the already saved draft-history source, filtering years <= 2024 BEFORE
materialization and <= each origin at join. Actual pick number and attached
school class only; no current biography or future ranking. Keep the latest
eligible selection for repeat draftees, explicitly not a certificate of signing.
Known flag, bounded log-pick rank, elapsed since draft, HS/JC/four-year/unknown
class, and pick-rank × recent-exposure reliability interaction. Unknown draft
evidence is unknown, not low pedigree or international bonus zero. Do not use
partially covered signing-dollar amounts. Record source duplicate/conflict checks,
coverage by year and actual thin-entry profile support. Kurtz's known selection
is legitimate origin information, not a manually prescribed boost.

## Fixed arms and fitting

1. Pooled history: retained V31 base context plus pooled event/exposure evidence.
2. Pooled + draft: identical features plus the above draft evidence.

Each arm has direct expected PA and direct expected delivered contribution heads,
using V31's fixed histogram learner settings and equal-origin training weights.
PA [0,800]; definitive ineligibility overrides to zero; zero predicted PA means
zero predicted contribution. No selected tail cap, retrospective recalibration
or tuned blend. Save separate actual-future-PA-weighted conditional batting-rate
tree heads as a diagnostic; score their product with each arm's workload but
do not confuse this contribution-weighted rate with pure current talent.

One additional linear diagnostic uses the pooled+draft representation and
Ridge(alpha=100), with declared fixed physical feature scales rather than
dividing by tiny learned rare-league standard deviations. Raw rates centered on
their priors and divided by .1; exposures divided by 600; MLB pooled quality in
wins/600; existing normalized context unchanged. This is a different regularized
representation, not an isolated test of changing one scaler. No output clipping
to make unsupported predictions look valid. Persist learned coefficients and
largest contributions; unsupported grades remain warnings, not useful ratings.

## Evaluation, review and disposition

Seven origins, all source stages, debut/absence/workload groups, equal-year
PA/value RMSE/MAE/bias, contribution-weighted batting-rate diagnostic, paired
player-cluster intervals and cohort totals. Primary broad comparison against
V31 direct_detail (same algorithm/eligibility); preserve stronger N/V24 references
with their different fitting histories and qualified public snapshot conversion.
Meaningful improved PA cannot conceal worse batting contribution, missed entry
profiles or 2021 league totals. No conditioning headline membership on future PA.

Fixed cases: Olson, McNeil, Steer, Winn, Judge, Rooker, McLain, Tatis, Ortiz,
Cruz, Eldridge, Kurtz, Made and De Vries where eligible. Add largest gain/harm,
false high/low and an ordinary case. Trace actual pooled numerators/denominators,
draft joins, fixed scales and predictions, and saved-fit pedigree-neutral probes
with origin-only peers. Complete mandatory player review before disposition.

This batch does not claim to fix finite-suspension or medical-state semantics,
foreign history, park/opponent adjustment or late-season jobs. Those omissions
remain explicit; do not flip a captured roster-list zero into certified rights.
Retain only useful components or a coherent candidate supported by the full
comparison. No goal-completion claim from a small pooled gain alone.
