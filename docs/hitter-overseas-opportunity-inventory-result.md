# Overseas opportunity inventory: reuse existing work, repair specific gaps

2026-10-05. Read-only diagnosis complete. No new fit, forecast, selection,
2026 evaluation or deployment. The [contract](hitter-overseas-opportunity-inventory-contract.md)
followed the completed [count-calibration review](hitter-foreign-count-calibration-player-review.md).
An implementation Path error stopped the first executable; its code/seal remain,
and the [documented recovery](hitter-overseas-opportunity-inventory-recovery.md)
changes only saved-path hashing, not sources, settings or comparison definitions.

## The professional-work idea was already implemented

The repaired evidence candidate's job heads already use professional PA,
last first-team work, previous MLB work/quality, dated employment, scouting,
age, position, domestic level histories, injuries and availability. Japan/Korea
PA are added to observed professional activity, not relabeled as equivalent MLB
talent. Its job heads do NOT use the seven translated foreign production terms
added to its separate hitting head. The older overseas integration DID feed raw
foreign exposure/production into its broader heads; repeating either setup is
not a new mechanism. Both complete candidates were previously rejected.

The current selected system retains its separate useful talent routes, rather
than this pooled replacement. The count experiment's weak fixed addition anchor
was deliberate component isolation, not the best newer opportunity prediction.
Confusing those anchors would falsely suggest that professional experience
had never been used. This inventory compares existing saved predictions only;
its exposed subgroup diagnostics do not approve a new combined model.

The source/model artifact is
`reports/generated/overseas-opportunity-inventory/inventory.json`. It keeps all
266 foreign-source forecast rows, 59 distinct focal/peer rows from the eighteen
previously selected player-origins, 118 exact two-head replays, all fitted job
inputs, source counts, tree-path terms, dated employment traces, literal roster
rows and actual held training support. Tree terms are exact numerical accounting,
not causal effects or validated feature ablations. The prior source/talent walks
remain linked, rather than selecting a new flattering set of players.

reconstruct every professional-activity feature for all 59 rows. The source
does not lack foreign work for these players; the earlier headline shortage is
often a learner/representation/support problem instead.

## Narrower next work—no automatic new test

1. Do not rebuild professional activity or rerun the broad overseas feature
   addition: both exist and have reviewed failures. Keep the stronger domestic
   talent routes and the existing job candidate as explicit separate anchors.
2. Inventory cutoff-known contract/role INTENT and foreign employment/exit
   evidence. Preserve legal agreement type, reserve membership, date/vintage,
   and whether the current MLB link is verified, stale, contradicted or unknown.
   Do not make an arbitrary expiry rule or infer rights from an MLB identifier.
   Young international professionals need an intended-opportunity fact distinct
   from contract form; Ohtani alone cannot teach a reliable interaction.
3. Audit existing public projection coverage for these international entrants.
   The prior 2,627 comparison deliberately requires prior-year MLB PA and has
   zero fresh-route overlap. It is NOT an external smell test for first-year
   overseas talent or workload. Search saved qualified exports by player identity,
   recording snapshot mismatch and missing rows instead of predicting zero.
   This source audit is useful before another fit; it cannot recertify 2026.
4. Only after those source/support checks predeclare a genuinely distinct,
   historical repair. Retain all non-arrivals and inspect PA/rate/value separately;
   no hand-selected subgroup blend or generic probability boost follows here.

Both frozen forecasts are unchanged; the reviewed 2026 result/explorer stays
final. The model's broad hitter/value goal remains active. This milestone prevents
repeating a completed idea and identifies actual source/support/semantics gaps;
it claims no predictive improvement.
