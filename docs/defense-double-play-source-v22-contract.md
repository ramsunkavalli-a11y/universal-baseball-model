# Check double play credit and training coverage before projecting skill

2026-10-07. The defense assembly omits a separately measured double-play channel.
Audit its saved MLB source and future-quality support before fitting anything.
This is a source/target check, not a forecasting comparison or a new winner.

The native position ledger contains separate double-play runs for infielders
from 2016 through 2025. Reconstruct them from the saved position responses,
checking requested years, identities, component additivity and official-out
certification. Keep null credit unknown; do not infer zero opportunities or
average talent from a non-null innings count. Record whether actual DP chances
and initial/pivot roles are available in those responses. Raw minor double-play
counts are not adjusted DP skill, and first-base receiving is a separate channel.

As a provisional coverage target only, pool same-position adjusted DP runs over
the next three calendar years in runs per 500 innings. Require 1,500 certified
outs, two measured seasons, complete follow-up through 2025, and no positive
official position exposure lacking qualified DP credit. Preserve every source
origin, including non-arrivals, short appearances and measurement gaps. This
target mixes adjusted conversion skill with traffic per inning; it is not a
mechanical conversion probability. No learner is authorized until that limit
and the actual opportunity denominator have been resolved or explicitly accepted
in a separate talent contract.

Use existing native MLB origins and no-prior/current-MLB-fielding minor origins
at 1B, 2B, 3B and SS, at least 25 current minor outs. For 2021/2022 forecast
cutoffs report distinct mature training people under every held-ID-fold exclusion,
position/level support, eligible and measurable test people, and unknown reasons.
No 2026 results, frozen forecast changes, feature tuning or accuracy scores.
The absence of a 2020 minor season is missing source time, not zero ability.

Fixed source cases are Semien, Giménez, Lindor, Arenado, Goldschmidt and Witt at
2022, plus Frick's minor 3B origin. Use three origin-known same-position peers
by age, log exposure and ID; never choose peers by later success. Show actual
numerators, innings and available opportunity fields, source qualification and
annual future paths without inventing fitted grades. Independently replay the
coverage counts and walk before choosing a model or another component experiment.

Tango's [DP method](https://tangotiger.com/index.php/site/article/statcast-dp-fielding-runs)
distinguishes initial and pivot fielders, uses time/difficulty information, and
has a pre-2020 versus later tracking adjustment. Its first version covers a
runner on first only with fewer than two outs. The
[Savant definition](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/fielding-run-value)
converts one additional DP to 0.4 runs, separately from range. The older
[UZR primer](https://blogs.fangraphs.com/the-fangraphs-uzr-primer/)
also separates DP conversion from ordinary range and accounts for ground-ball
speed/location. These are reasons to audit the denominator and scope, not proof
that our season counts contain the same information.
