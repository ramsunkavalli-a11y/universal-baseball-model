# Historical MLB use now separates old restrictions from continuing absence

2026-10-05. A separate observation adapter repairs a real source defect without
rewriting legal history or changing a projection. It uses already archived MLB
appearance intervals, not future participation or named-player overrides.

All 83,300 player origins are retained. Of 485 origins with captured active
restrictions for 154 people, 155 origins for 38 people have strictly later MLB
appearance evidence. In the current model source this affects 122 origins; in
the fixed historical evaluation it affects 89 forecasts for 28 people. All 83
earlier conservative annual warnings are covered. The additional 72 population
origins include same-year returns and people outside the scored hitter cohort.
These are counts of corrected observation meaning, not accuracy gains.

Forty-five raw MLB regular-season captures for 2010–2024 reconstruct exactly
against the stored appearance windows, with 49,542 PA checks. Every positive
window is tied to its request interval and source hash. A window must start
strictly after the latest active event in that channel and end by the exact
information cutoff. Overlaps, later restrictions and different channels do not
silently clear. The adapter records only an upper bound on observed return.

Captured legal restrictions, original sentences and tentative reports remain
unchanged. Separate observation fields distinguish unresolved absence from
subsequent observed MLB use. After observed use, old sentence/report timing is
not presented as current absence timing. No positive window is uncertainty,
not proof of continued absence. Death/permanent bans are never cleared by PA;
unexpected activity would create a source-conflict warning. There are no such
hard-status conflicts in this population.

## Limits and next step

This is observation evidence, not certified legal reinstatement, medical recovery,
exact missed days, remaining suspension games or a current role. Historical
captures are retrospective, not publication-vintage-certified. The positive-only
adapter does not turn missing observations into healthy/unavailable status, and
does not provide all-level minor-league return coverage. The saved field
`appearance_capture_years` describes archive scope, not complete elapsed-year
availability at a January cutoff; eligibility is always enforced by each
window's end/availability date.

No model was fitted, no forecast/explorer was revised, and the completed 2026
evaluation was not reopened. Keep this source repair. Next predeclare one
matched historical opportunity comparison with fixed hitting and coherent
dependent status/timing fields, existing anchors and public benchmark cohorts.
Check actual rare-profile support, PA/probability/value losses, origins/stages,
totals and retained player misses before deciding whether coefficients improve.
Do not repeat closed employment variants or the old broad injury sweep.
