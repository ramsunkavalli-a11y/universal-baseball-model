# Full hitter value: retain the component assembly, qualify prospect calibration

October 9, 2026. This closes the fixed historical integration check, including
the appended positive-PA reference correction and the prospect investigation.
It does not certify six-year control values or authorize a finished release.

The reference correction changes no forecast by 0.001 WAR. Every identity,
playing-time estimate, skill estimate, role and outcome is unchanged. Original
results are preserved. Public PA totals now use each public system's own PA;
only verified WAR/PA columns enter the normalized actual-value label table.

## What the comparison establishes

For 1,985 current-MLB player-seasons matched to saved 2023–25 Steamer forecasts,
UBM full-WAR RMSE is 1.153 and average absolute error .761 WAR; Steamer is
1.164 and .772. The player-clustered RMSE difference interval is −.049 to +.025.
Call this broadly comparable on the available development archives, not a win
over Steamer. Original public-forecast timestamps remain qualified. Plain ZiPS
is not a depth-chart playing-time forecast and is not the opportunity benchmark.

On all 12,432 forecasts, combined RMSE .4823 improves on .4953 for simple
recent full-WAR history at identical expected PA and .5187 for batting plus
replacement alone. The latter is an accounting diagnostic, not a competing
full-WAR system. The primary practical error guardrails pass. The 2023/24/25
WAR totals are approximately 539/561/552 against 568/570/570: reasonable overall
but not evidence that every level or profile is calibrated. No league quota is
imposed. Most all-player rows are non-arriving minors; use the current-MLB
comparison to describe accuracy for major leaguers.

## Why the upper-minors total is high

The group projects 94.23 WAR versus 64.94 actual over three seasons, despite PA
being only 4.2% high (44,484 versus 42,699). Adding defense/position/running
reduces the batting-only anchor's 99.21 WAR, so the excess did not originate
with the new defensive assembly. Most excess is in 2024: 37.00 versus 16.68
WAR, with PA 16,532 versus 13,027. In 2023 totals are 29.01 versus 26.63;
in 2025, 28.22 versus 21.63. This is a real calibration limitation.

Verified actual component fields reconcile to published runs above replacement
within 1e-8 runs in all three years. Framing is already included in the source's
Fielding column, so it is not added again. Across upper-minors forecasts UBM
batting is −394 runs versus −718 observed; fielding is approximately +10 versus
−49. Replacement is +1,353 versus +1,306, while running and the common league
reference offset part of those differences. These are attribution diagnostics,
not like-for-like talent losses: exposure, park treatment and component
definitions differ. They nevertheless rule out a huge invented catcher bonus
as the principal explanation.

"Upper minors" describes last-season activity, not never having played MLB:
410 rows are MLB returnees, including Tatis. They project 6,555 PA/8.92 WAR
against 3,954/.46. The 2,432 never-MLB rows project 37,929 PA/85.31 WAR against
38,745/64.48. Keep those populations separate in explorer explanations.

Players who subsequently receive PA were forecast for 22,114 PA but actually
received 42,699; those without subsequent PA carried 22,370 expected PA. This
split is conditioned on future outcomes and is NOT itself evidence of a bad
probability model—some expectation must belong to unsuccessful candidates.
Do not zero those players with hindsight. The relevant issue is the total,
the origin-defined subgroups and the named source-to-forecast failures.

## Prospect player calculations

- Lawlar before 2025: 490 AA/AAA PA and a 34-PA MLB debut in 2023, then only
  104 minor PA in 2024. Forecast 331 PA and −.131 batting wins/600 yields −.72
  batting, +10.02 replacement, +2.73 position and +.35 range runs, or 1.40 WAR.
  Actual is −.46 in 74 PA. The large miss is opportunity/production, not an
  excessive range award. Prior-MLB shortstop peers Basabe, Downs and Barrero
  have 0/0/31 subsequent PA; Lawlar's optimistic opportunity deserves a flag,
  not a hindsight injury or demotion override.
- Holliday before 2024: 581 PA across A/High-A/AA/AAA at age 19, including
  91 AAA PA. Forecast 351 PA and +.709 batting wins/600 gives +4.15 batting,
  +10.90 replacement, +3.06 position and −.99 range runs: 1.69 WAR versus
  −.04 in 208 PA. This is a young prospect struggling initially, not proof
  of no talent. Arroyo/Williams/Bonaci comparisons do not reach MLB that year.
- Merrill before 2024: 511 High-A/AA PA, 15 HR and only 62 strikeouts at age
  20. Forecast 142 PA and −.175 batting wins/600 awards −.41 batting, +4.41
  replacement, +1.05 position and +.14 range: .51 WAR versus 5.36 in 593 PA.
  Both fast arrival and hitting are missed; the origin shortstop role also
  does not anticipate his MLB center-field role. Bonaci/Amador/Vera are the
  coarse source-selected peers; only Amador plays, for −.40 WAR. Those peers
  are not close scouting matches and cannot prove the forecast sound.
- Kurtz before 2025: only 35 A-ball and 15 AA PA, four HR and 12 walks. The
  reliability-repaired batting rate is +.145 wins/600 but expected PA only 10.
  +.02 batting, +.31 replacement and −.19 position give .016 WAR versus 4.28
  in 489 PA. Strong advanced amateurs remain a known arrival blind spot.
  De Los Santos/Encarnacion/Long do not arrive; their broad position/age match
  is not a substitute for pedigree and readiness. Do not lower all prospects
  to repair the cohort total, which would deepen this miss.
- Mayo before 2024: 614 AA/AAA PA and 29 HR. Forecast 225 PA and +.433
  batting wins/600 gives +1.62 batting, +6.99 replacement, −.71 position and
  +.11 range: .77 WAR versus −.57 in 46 PA. Again, timing and first-year
  hitting dominate. Rodríguez/Bernabel/Ramos peers include two nonarrivals
  and a negative-value arrival, not uniformly successful prospects.
- Baldwin before 2025: 551 AA/AAA PA, 16 HR, 72 walks and 95 strikeouts.
  Forecast 214 PA and −.420 batting wins/600 produces .53 WAR against 3.16
  in 446 PA. The small +.17 positional award is traceable: his current minor
  record contains 75 catcher and 48 DH starts; the allocator carries about
  60% catcher/40% DH, producing 877 catching outs and 22 DH starts. Actual
  2025 has 2,279 catcher outs and 12 DH starts. This is a documented role
  translation weakness, not missing recorded catching innings. Unmeasured
  framing remains an explicit neutral prior. Valenzuela/Florentino/Mujica do
  not arrive; these are broad age/position peers, not high-quality comparables.
- Valdez before 2023 is an ordinary success: 573 AA/AAA PA and 28 HR become
  148 expected MLB PA, −.449 batting wins/600, −1.11 batting, +4.41 replacement
  and approximately −.11 net position/range runs. Forecast .323 WAR versus
  .346 in 149 PA. A comparable chosen without outcomes, Julien, instead
  reaches 408 PA and 2.63 WAR versus .118 expected; the uncertainty is real.
- Tatis's suspended/interrupted-season failure remains the already documented
  open baseline issue, not a new defense failure. No silent player override.

Exact source stints, saved batting inputs, role calculations and outcome-blind
peers are in the cohort-walk receipt. The peer rule is deliberately simple and
too coarse for rare elite/medical profiles; it illustrates missing support,
not independent validation of those cases.

## Decision for v1

Retain the combined component assembly. It materially improves full-value
accounting, meets the practical matched error guardrails and is not terrible
against the available MLB benchmark. Qualify young/returning players rather
than applying an untested discount: upper-minors calibration, fast advanced
arrivals, catcher-to-DH translation, elite batting tails and finite medical/
suspension returns remain visible limitations. Lower-minors near-term totals
(6.54 versus 1.62 WAR) have very little opportunity and do not validate eventual
talent or justify precise long-range dollars.

Player review complete; development component assembly retained. Current rights,
service, contract tails, supported annual paths and a verified explorer still
must be completed before the overall goal or release can be called finished.
