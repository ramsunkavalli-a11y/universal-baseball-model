# Complete employment correction does not establish an accuracy gain

2026-10-05. Keep the corrected signing and assignment source definitions, but
do not promote the refitted playing-time model. The complete matched comparison
now repairs all twelve employment-dependent inputs, including evidence age and
unknown-date fields. It does not produce a demonstrated improvement. This
closes the source-correction test; it is not another invitation to try variants.

## What changed and what stayed fixed

The earlier [limited result](hitter-employment-comparison-result.md) remains
intact. Its player review found that timing fields still used the old source
dates. The appended [amendment](hitter-employment-comparison-timing-amendment.md)
allows those two dependent fields to change, but no feature names, settings,
training identities, labels or hitting predictions. All five source matrices
retain 63,314 rows; employment inputs change at 10,276 source rows and 7,125
evaluated originals. Latest source dates change at 10,267 source rows. These
counts differ because some date changes are capped and flag changes need not
change the date. The limited test's affected group contained 4,338 originals;
its subgroup result is not a like-for-like comparison with this expanded group.

All 30,506 original forecasts and thirteen additions remain across seven
origins and five whole-player folds. Seventy baseline heads had replayed before
fitting, 140 old/corrected full/active prefit checks were persisted, and seventy
new heads fit and replay. The target remains next year's actual MLB PA and
batting plus replacement, not full WAR, control years or trade value. Target
2020 stays excluded; short-season history and canceled minor leagues are not
converted into ordinary zero seasons. No 2026 outcomes enter this development
comparison and the completed final 2026 evaluation is unchanged.

## Totals and public comparison

Original expected PA are 1,236,263 versus 1,270,493 actual across seven seasons;
expected contribution 4,182.94 versus 4,185.43. Those close pooled value totals
are not adequate calibration. Never-debut upper-minors players receive 74,213
PA versus 92,891 actual, and 176.56 contribution versus 196.73. Lower minors
receive 8,108 PA versus 5,194 and 18.34 contribution versus 9.48. The 2021
origin still overpredicts contribution 614.09 versus 568.34 while underpredicting
PA 167,350 versus 181,583. This is not solved by more accurate employment dates.

The thirteen additions receive 743 PA and 2.63 expected participants versus
1,625 PA and six actual. Their fixed research fallback talent is unchanged;
they have no incumbent forecast, not a zero benchmark. Across all 266 foreign
forecasts, expected PA 9,272 look close to 9,401 actual while contribution
23.66 misses 41.74. Player-level roles and talent remain weak despite the sum.

On the unchanged 2,627 public matches, corrected PA RMSE is 137.77 versus
Steamer 135.38, and MAE 105.28 versus 92.08. This meets the existing working
tolerances but is still 14.34% worse in average workload error. Contribution
RMSE 1.025 versus 1.090 uses the existing qualified public rate conversion;
uncertain publication days, park and rate-reference differences prevent a
claim of overall superiority. Ordinary ZiPS PA is not unconditional workload.
The separate overseas public-coverage audit remains necessary; missing exports
are unknown, not zero. Hitting accuracy itself cannot improve in this test
because both job arms use exactly the same talent prediction.
