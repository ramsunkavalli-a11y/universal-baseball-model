# Preserve production when historical scouting adds no positive evidence

2026-10-03, before evaluating the new assembly. The ranking experiment and all
fifteen model/nine source walks are complete. Its repeated new-draftee and rising-
prospect harms justify one explicit information-applicability repair, not a
parameter search or another refit. This design is motivated by exposed historical
results and is development evidence; its intervals are not independent confirmation.

## Fixed rule and target

On the exact same 30,506 origins/players, use the saved ranking-head PA when the
current historical list contains a verified positive rank for the player.
Otherwise retain the count/games PA exactly. A historical absence, partial-list
unknown or future ranking is not positive readiness evidence. No manual player
exceptions, draft-pick cutoffs, fitted blending weights or outcome-dependent
eligibility. This does not manufacture end-of-year ranks for Kurtz, Langford,
Bellinger or Alonso, or improve their original count-only forecasts by fiat.
It prevents the new source from taking away the old forecast when it adds no
positive player-specific scouting evidence. The ranking head still learned
from the full original training population, including unlisted players.

Predict next-calendar-year MLB PA including zero outcomes, and multiply by the
exact unchanged V34/V38 PA-weighted future-MLB batting head plus origin replacement.
The target is delivered offense/replacement, not full WAR, present-day scouting
talent, service/control years, trade value or a calibrated risk distribution.
Policies and all old comparison arms stay unchanged. No 2026 outcomes, new college
data, production/frozen forecast or deployed explorer edits.

## Verification and evaluation

Save source/prediction/verification/contract hashes and all gate identities before
calculating losses. The 35 original heads already replay; verify those evidence
hashes and exact old columns, source joining and formula again. Positive rank
selection uses origin source inputs only, not next-year participation or outcomes.
Changing future outcomes cannot change the gate. Current positive rank can remain
stale even for a graduated MLB hitter; show Seager rather than silently adding a
second career-exposure rule after seeing this test.

Primary equal-origin PA MSE and contribution MSE versus games; working assembly
and unmodified ranking candidate separately. Report MAE, nominal player-cluster
intervals, fixed public matches, seven origins, four stages, listed/top20,
never-debut upper minors, brief debuts and cohort totals. Retain existing practical
public tolerances. The lower-minor count-only excess cannot be declared repaired
by fallback, and a mean does not quantify the chance of an all-star outcome.

Review the eight prior fixed cases and all fifteen prior diagnosed cases, plus
any new largest gain/harm, false high/low and ordinary cases. Carry their actual
stats/inputs/path/support forward, record which head the gate chose, and show
control/candidate/actual PA, rate and contribution with origin-selected peers.
Newly selected cases receive the same full path/source review, not a name list.
Do not tune to those names. Close the decision only after every case is reviewed.

If gains survive the fallback and the harms are understood, retain this coherent
assembly for research comparison, but do not waive public-MAE, cohort or hitting-
talent limits. The next substantive choice must address a documented remaining
mechanism rather than improve the appearance of one pooled score. Do not mark
the practical goal complete without its actual standards being met.
