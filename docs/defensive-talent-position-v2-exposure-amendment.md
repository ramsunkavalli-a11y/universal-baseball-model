# Exposure agreement: pre-fit reasonability correction

2026-10-06. No model has been fitted. Preserve the exact-agreement source report
and preparation as conservative anchors. This amendment changes annual measurement
certification, identically for both model arms, before predictions are inspected.

The extension found 306 infield position-season disagreements across 2016–2025.
Every difference is at most five outs; examples include Lindor 2016 (4,092 versus
4,094), Arenado 2016 (4,131 versus 4,132), and Abrams 2023 (one out). Exact equality
was useful for the initial two-year pilot but is not a sensible blanket reason
to discard thousands of innings. This could itself distort training selection.

Use the native position denominator, which agrees exactly with the same-vintage
native player total, and independently qualify it against official usage if the
absolute difference is at most five outs **and** at most 1% of the larger count.
Positive exposure, finite non-null range, embedded year/position and recomposition
requirements remain unchanged. Both counts and their discrepancies remain in
the saved data. Five outs is not assumed to be rounding, nor is either source
declared erroneous; the cause of their counting discrepancy remains unresolved.
This is a bounded exposure cross-check, not a correction to published range runs.

At the 1% ceiling, substituting one denominator for the other can change a range
rate by only about 1%, far smaller than typical player-quality noise. Tiny stints
with proportionally large disagreement remain uncertified. Missing/null range
is still unknown, never inferred as zero from aggregate recomposition.

Write qualified data, context and labels in a new subdirectory; do not replace
the completed strict preparation. Report strict and qualified support counts.
No result-based tolerance tuning or second model search is authorized.
