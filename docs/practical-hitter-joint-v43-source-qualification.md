draft, known age, position, name or club; target 2025 has 170 MLB PA. His identity
came from the older full-roster cohort. A date parameter alone was insufficient
proof of a cutoff-observable affiliated membership. He could be considered in a
global international-player universe from other dated evidence, but this source
does not establish that universe or supply his foreign performance. Do not call
him a validated inactive affiliated hitter or assign historical Dodgers ownership.

headline: any observed pre-cutoff batting history in the preserved source,
known MLB debut by cutoff, or cutoff-eligible Rule 4 selection. The selection is

signees can also fall in the unverified group. Do not delete them silently.
