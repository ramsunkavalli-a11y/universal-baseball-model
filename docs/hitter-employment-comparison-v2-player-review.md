2026-10-05. Correcting assignment records and their derived dates fixes source
meaning, but does not fix the main playing-time misses. This review closes the
bounded comparison without promoting its refitted opportunity model. Hitting
forecasts are identical in both arms. All targets are next-calendar-year MLB
PA and batting plus replacement, not full WAR or club-control value.

Machado's KBO seasons contain 539 and 560 PA. The March 17, 2022 assignment

changes employment evidence age from one day to 79 days, removes false signed
first-team work (.898333 to zero), and preserves the actual professional work.

model path contributions, not literal job guarantees. The source is now correct,
but workload error worsens. Lowering predicted positive batting contribution
toward a small negative actual contribution does not establish improved talent.
There are eight exact participation profile people and zero conditional people.

Bourgeois's 238 AAA PA in 2017, 483 in 2016 and 212 MLB PA in 2015 remain.

signed-work input becomes zero. Old fixed parameters give 1.86 PA; the corrected

Removing the false linked work is the principal mechanical reduction. The older
employment date contributes negatively to participation in this fit. Only two
conditional profile people support this intersection. Olivo similarly moves
7.39 to 6.22 versus zero, with 313 AAA PA in 2016, corrected evidence age
.8575 to 1.0000 years and two conditional profile people. Neither requires
pretending an assignment proves no job or that an old agreement certifies
current rights.

## Cohorts and decision

The accompanying [result](hitter-employment-comparison-v2-result.md) retains all
30,506 originals and thirteen additions. Close pooled contribution totals
still hide upper-minors underallocation, lower-minors overallocation and the
2021-origin rate/workload mismatch. Exact conditional support is zero for 4,880
forecasts and below twenty for 19,205. None is removed from scoring.

Player walkthrough is complete, but the candidate does not show a demonstrated
primary gain. Keep the source correction, preserve both limited and complete
comparisons, and close this contrast without another variant. No frozen model,
explorer or completed 2026 evaluation changes. Before another modeling test,
inventory the existing availability work and actual cutoff-known restriction
timing. Tatis and Franco motivate that narrowly defined source/design review;
they are not permission to tune to their outcomes. Foreign roles and elite
prospect readiness remain explicit unresolved boundaries, not silently solved.
