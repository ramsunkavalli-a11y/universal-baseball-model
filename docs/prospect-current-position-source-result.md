# Prospect current-position source result

Status: source hierarchy accepted for private position research.

Official 2026 fielding outs cover 2,591 of the 2,805 pre-MLB hitters in the position-
value sensitivity (92.4%). The corrected games-based role covers the remaining 214.
No player requires the listed-position final fallback in this population.

Among fielding-covered players, the fielding-out group agrees with the games-based
role for 2,407 of 2,591 players (92.9%). It agrees with MLBAM's listed primary-position
group for 1,928 of 2,385 comparable players (80.8%). This supports the frozen order:
fielding outs first, games role second, listed position last.

This selection is about source quality, not player value. It adds no position bonus or
penalty and does not alter the playable default. Future position research should use
this hierarchy consistently instead of mixing a games-derived transition origin with
a listed-position label without disclosure.
