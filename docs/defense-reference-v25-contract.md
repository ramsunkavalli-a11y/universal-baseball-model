# Reconcile outfield ability with positional value

2026-10-07. This accounting audit follows the reviewed older-quality source.
Raw native outfield range compares outfielders across positions; the published
position schedule assumes fielding credit relative to each position. Determine
the effect of mixing these references in the existing research assembly before
using it for WAR-like valuation or linking older measurements.

## Scope and definitions

Retain all 13,304 native annual records and all 12,432 saved player origins at
2022–2024, targeting 2023–2025. No fit, parameter search, new source capture,
2026 selection, explorer change or frozen forecast alteration. Prior skill and
opportunity verdicts remain separate. The old expanded common-win target was
explicitly custom; an accounting difference does not invalidate every old score.

Only LF, CF and RF range receive position reference offsets. Infield, catcher,
arm and receiving credits are unchanged. Do not force every channel to zero or
claim an exact FanGraphs WAR reconstruction. Catcher definitions, missing source
coverage, DH-start approximation and fixed ten runs per win remain limitations.

For each observed season and outfield position, compute the exposure-weighted
center from ALL qualified native records: 1,500 times total range runs divided
by total native outs. No minimum-innings survivor filter. Record qualified people,
native outs, total official outs, unmatched official exposure and source gaps.
Subtract the center times each record's native outs / 1,500 for measured
position-relative range. Keep original intrinsic range, native outs, official
outs and positional adjustment in separate fields. Missing positive-exposure
measurement remains null; certified absence is zero delivered runs, not skill.
These realized seasonal centers are descriptive accounting, never forecast inputs.

For a usable cutoff-only conversion, separately compute each origin/fold center
from the existing last three seasons with weights 1, 1/2, 1/4; exclude every
player with ID modulo five equal to the evaluation fold across all positions
and seasons. This is a fixed reference estimate, not a fitted talent candidate.
Keep its source seasons, distinct people, exposure and held-ID exclusion checks.
Do not use actual future baselines or counts in a forecast conversion.

Intrinsic forecast rate minus cutoff center gives a reference-conversion
diagnostic. Unknown talent stays unknown: the legacy numerical zero is an
unknown-mean fallback in the raw reference, not proof of average-position skill.
A neutral-position fallback must explicitly correspond to the cutoff center in
raw coordinates. Do not silently substitute that policy in saved forecasts.
Also expose the prior issue: shrinking raw OF range toward zero and then moving
the reference is not generally the same as shrinking toward position average.
No new reliability or recency sweep follows from this audit.

## Exact comparison and checks

rows from saved rates, denominators and actual measurements. Independently
recompute positional adjustment and existing custom value. Demonstrate a change
of coordinates can preserve value by moving the offset from fielding to position
credit, and that removing the offset instead changes the valuation definition.
Never present pure re-expression as an accuracy improvement.

For each origin and opportunity arm, show the frozen cutoff offset implied by
projected LF/CF/RF exposure, its position breakdown, and corresponding realized
offset using native exposure. Include missing measured-range coverage and the
complete-defined subset separately. Do not score new RMSE, bootstrap or choose
a reference because an exposed outcome score is better. The accounting basis
must follow the baseball definition, not loss minimization.

Tests must cover exposure weighting instead of averaging player rates, reference
reversibility, unchanged within-position rankings, zero versus missing exposure,
unknown quality versus neutral-position prior, chronology and held-person
exclusion, and unchanged infield/other channels. All saved identities and unknown
targets remain in the audit. No silent row drops.
