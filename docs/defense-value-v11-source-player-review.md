# What the defensive records say about individual players

The reviewed ledger separates observed defensive skill, projected opportunities
and measured future runs. It is ready for a matched integration comparison,
not a claim that the combined forecast is accurate. Minor-league position
records establish roles; they do not supply measured MLB range or framing talent.

## Coverage and calculations

Across the 2023, 2024 and 2025 target seasons, the fixed population contains
644, 638 and 661 actual MLB defenders. The reviewed twelve-channel definition
is completely measured for 539, 525 and 561 of them. Another 105, 113 and 100
remain partial. Most missing range records are tiny secondary-position stints;
mixed-position arm measurement also remains incomplete. No partial sum is
called a complete defensive outcome. Non-arrivals have zero delivered opportunity,
not an observed neutral talent grade.

Three years of eligible MLB evidence receive weights 1, 0.5 and 0.25. Each
channel directly shrinks weighted runs toward zero by adding a fixed prior
exposure: range 3,000 outs, framing 6,000 pitches, throwing 100 attempts,
blocking 3,000 opportunities, arms 300 opportunities, receiving 600 throws.
Range rates are per 1,500 outs; framing/blocking per 1,000 opportunities;
throwing/arm/receiving per 100. Those units must be converted before summing runs.

The separate saved range age calibration remains available, with its actual
chronological and player-exclusion provenance and profile warnings. It is not
invented for a minor leaguer with no MLB range history. The following rates use
the transparent history unless stated otherwise. The full source records,
eligible seasons, weighted arithmetic, all positions, missing channels and
projected opportunities are in the
[36 preserved case records](../reports/model-evidence/defense-value-v11/reviewed-source-player-cases.json).
Peers were selected by origin, stage, dominant role, age and exposure, before
looking at outcomes. No favorable replacement was made.

## Center field histories do not settle future playing time

At the 2022 origin, Kiermaier has 3,052 weighted CF outs and +6.745 weighted
range runs. His history estimate is 1,500 × 6.745 / 6,052 = +1.672 runs per
500 innings; the saved age calibration gives +1.807. His projected 1,214 CF
outs are far below the subsequent 2,944, in which he records +10.152 range runs.
Only 202 expected PA versus 408 actual leaves a material opportunity miss.
His arm history is +0.943 per 100 chances, but subsequent arm value is only
+0.092 runs on 274 opportunities. Good range and good arm history are not
interchangeable components, and one year does not establish the same stability.

## New prospects need an honest unknown talent fallback

At the 2023 origin, Chourio has no measured MLB range history. The fallback
mean is zero, flagged unknown. His minor CF role informs possible positions,
but the forecast has 1,109 CF outs against none actual and too few corner outs.
The subsequent LF/RF measurements are -0.925/+6.095 runs, with +1.329 OF arm
runs, for a +6.499 defined total. Those future measurements cannot be used
to supply his origin-known grade.

Anthony and Calabrese also lack MLB skill history and do not arrive in the
next target season. Wood does arrive: 336 actual PA against 188 expected and
2,093 LF outs, with -4.743 LF range runs. The identical unknown-quality fallback
must accommodate both Chourio and Wood, not pretend it identifies their
different talent. Their very different later records are an explicit lower-minors
transfer gap, not grounds for awarding all minor CFs a positive MLB grade.

Eldridge at the 2024 origin likewise has no measured MLB 1B range or receiving
history. Forecast 1B exposure is 254 outs versus 102 actual, with 40.9 forecast
received throws versus 24 actual. His observed +0.005 range and +0.438 receiving
runs are tiny samples, not established strong defensive talent. Isaac, Clifford
and Ariza have the same unknown-quality status and no target-season MLB fielding.
Their zero contribution does not validate the fallback as a talent estimate.

## Catcher evidence must use real pitches and attempts

Álvarez's 95 origin framing pitches contain -0.035 runs. Direct shrinkage gives
only -0.0057 per 1,000 pitches, correctly weak evidence. His next 8,053 pitches
produce +13.946 framing runs. There is no eligible throwing history; the
fallback does not identify the later -2.804 throwing runs across 82 attempts.
Blocking history from 59 opportunities yields +0.064 per 1,000; later blocking
is +0.063 delivered runs. Summing the three measured channels gives +11.204.
The 6,351 forecast pitches also undershoot actual exposure.

## Moving out of catcher changes where skill can earn runs

Varsho's 2022 history gives CF +2.507 and RF +2.786 per 500 innings, versus
LF +0.326 from much less exposure. His framing history is -0.331 per 1,000
pitches. The research position forecast still assigns 597 C outs and many RF
outs, while he actually records 2,453 LF and 1,387 CF outs, with no C or RF
exposure. Actual CF range is +7.524, LF +0.942, OF arms +2.533. His defined
total is +10.999; a catcher rate must earn no actual runs when he does not catch.

Castro's future defined total is +3.126 despite weak near-neutral origin OF
range evidence and no qualified isolated OF arm history. Tucker's positive
RF and arm histories reverse to a -4.460 defined total. Eaton's record remains
partial, with 56 actual PA against 185 expected; it must not become a complete
zero-filled score. These failures stay beside the positive Varsho example.

## First base receiving and range must not be counted twice

Olson's range history is +0.372 per 500 innings, but subsequent range is
-3.707 runs. His separate receiving history is much more persistent:
100 × 7.738 / (1,050.5 + 600) = +0.469 per 100 throws. The next 675 throws
produce +4.187 runs. Their sum is only +0.480, not +4.187 plus the native
aggregate total. Forecast receiving opportunities are 618 versus 675 actual.

Walsh's range/receiving estimates are -0.560/+0.006 in their respective units,
but actual delivered components are -1.285/-1.744. His 381 expected PA against
116 actual show a large workload error. Harold Castro's projected 673 1B outs
do not occur; he plays other positions and his defined total is -4.856.
Jones records no target-year fielding despite positive tiny receiving history.

## What this supports next

All nine focal and 27 peer source walks are reviewed. The independent replay
checks 149,184 channel rows, all fixed official exposure labels and all history
calculations. Sixty-five previously unknown OF totals are recovered by measured
pure-OF evidence, not by zero filling; complete participant coverage increases
modestly and genuine gaps remain. Both initial and corrected artifacts are kept.

Proceed to one predeclared comparison of neutral versus these fixed skill
histories, with the existing position/opportunity alternatives and unchanged
batting forecasts. Inspect delivered defensive runs separately from expanded
player value, and show missing-measurement coverage beside scores. No talent,
WAR, lower-minors transfer or deployment improvement is claimed by this source
review. The frozen forecast and explorer are unchanged.
