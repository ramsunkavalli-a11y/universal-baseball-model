## Fixed structural comparisons

1. **Base hurdle:** four future states (0, 1–199, 200–399, 400+ MLB PA), predicting their probabilities, then expected PA and delivered batting-plus-replacement value within each playing state. Inputs: age/known debut, recent normalized MLB and level workloads, observed MLB quality, source position, missingness and reconstructed list context. Direct conditional value heads let performance and opportunity remain related within each state; do not multiply unrelated talent and playing-time averages.
2. **Detailed hurdle:** identical structure plus three years of separate-level K, walks, HBP, HR, BABIP, doubles/triples, denominators and recency/change. Fixed 100-opportunity priors stabilize representations without treating lower-league rates as MLB rates. Learn the relationship to future MLB outcomes from training only.
3. **Detailed direct:** predict expected PA and expected delivered batting value directly on the same population/features; keep zero cases. This is a substantively different integration from a hurdle.
4. **Talent diagnostics:** standardized ridge (alpha 100) and histogram boosting predict future MLB batting runs converted to wins per 600 PA above that target year's MLB average. Learn on next-year active players only, once with equal-player/year weights (conditional-active performance), once with future-PA weights (contribution-weighted rate). The latter estimates a different mean that weights performance by opportunity; it is not pure current talent. A fixed recency/shrinkage MLB-only floor is retained, not a MiLB translation. Compare rate and delivered value with the same workload on the V24 intersection and public hitting-rate benchmarks. A conditional rate win cannot certify never-arrived players' current MLB ability.

All tree heads: histogram gradient boosting, 250 iterations, depth 3, min leaf 30, rate .05, L2 10, no random validation/early stopping; same family selected for practicality after V30's broadly similar boosted results, not a claimed uniquely best library. Equal-origin training weights; conditional subsets inherit them. PA [0,800]. Values have no arbitrary upper tail cap. No new hyperparameter sweep. Save every fitted head/model, inputs and forecast intermediate; verify numerical replay.
