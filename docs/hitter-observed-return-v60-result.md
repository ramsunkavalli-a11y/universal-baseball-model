# Correct continuing-absence evidence before using it for workload

2026-10-03. Source-only repair; no fitted predictive model or forecast change.
Corrected V53 remains the research baseline, and the practical hitter goal stays
active. Protected 2026 stays closed.

The new independent observation adapter distinguishes a reported injury from
continuing MLB roster absence. A recognized MLB roster return or a positive PA
window strictly after the latest injury placement/transfer ends the continuing
absence observation. It does not certify health, a starting role or the exact
first game back. Later placements reopen separate observations.

Of 81 source player-years formerly marked open, 48 now have known observed
closure, representing 41 people. Among the 72 evaluation player-years, 45 close.
The previous strict-window audit identified 47 source/44 evaluation contradictions;
the additional closure is Riley Greene's November 6, 2023 reserve-list activation.
His zero late PA did not prove continuing absence through December.

All 63,282 original source identities are retained. Missing captured medical
scope remains null/unknown, not an assertion of health. The adapter reports a
conservative possible-absence upper bound and preserves placement history.
Those bounds must not be substituted for measured injury days. No observed
return certifies complete recovery. Original forecasts and batting rates are
unchanged; eight future-version probes cannot change origin state.

## Disposition and next material comparison

Retain the adapter as corrected **observation-state evidence**, with explicit
bound/coverage semantics. Do not promote the earlier V59 coefficients or rerun
the same ten-feature sweep as though duration were now measured exactly.

The next workload design must distinguish persistent opportunity from temporary
interruption and recent brief use, keeping ordinary unsuccessful peers. Earlier
four-state and direct-workload tests are existing anchors, not novel ideas to
repeat under a new name. Audit their target/support assumptions when choosing
one substantive comparison; keep batting fixed and assess delivered offense,
public errors, full cohort totals and actual player paths together.

milestone. The current explorer's corrected V53 forecasts remain the default.
