## Source rules

Keep two separate records: captured legal evidence and observed continuing
absence. A positive window can contradict continuing absence in a channel only
if its start is strictly after that channel's latest active restriction event
and its end is no later than the exact information cutoff. Use the earliest
eligible window end as an upper bound on observed return, not an exact date.
Do not assume absence continued to that end or began again afterward.

An overlapping or earlier window cannot resolve a newer restriction. Replay the
existing legal ledger first, including scoped activations and parallel lists;
an observed return must not erase a later same-channel entry or another channel
whose date is newer. Positive PA does not certify legal reinstatement, full health,
remaining suspension games, current rights or a guaranteed job. Preserve original
duration and tentative return reports as history; do not reuse them as timing of
a currently continuing absence once later play contradicts that interpretation.

Death and permanent ineligibility are never cleared by appearances. Unexpected
positive activity after either is a source conflict requiring investigation, not
permission to remove a hard rule. No positive window means no observed contradiction,
not proof of continuing restriction. Missing appearance scope remains unknown.
Medical and employment evidence, original forecasts and all source identities are
unchanged. New observation flags must have explicit names distinct from legal flags.
