# Repair evidence weighting in the practical hitter model

2026-10-04. This is the next implementation plan after the completed overseas
comparison and all 38 player walks. It does not approve new predictions or change
the broad goal. The objective remains a useful future-MLB hitting, playing-time
and delivered-value candidate, with a team-filtered research explorer.

## Problem to solve

We now have Japanese/Korean professional production and useful domestic history.
The failed integration lets sparse, correlated rate columns overpower recent MLB
production; job models still confuse foreign professional experience and finite
absence with generic missing US history. Separate evidence-quality and availability
problems must not be covered up by a smaller pooled RMSE or an optimistic rate
canceling too little PA.

The scale failure is broader than Japan/Korea. A 57-PA older DSL walk line drives
Yordan's apparent biggest gain; Benintendi's old short-season walks help drive the
biggest domestic harm. This rules out another overseas-only coefficient tweak as
a sufficient remedy. It does not prove a preferred replacement yet.

## Implementation sequence

First inventory and replay the strongest current talent branches on the exact
original population. Identify their feature transforms, targets and routing,
not merely their filenames or summary scores. Determine which missing-player
forecasts can be reconstructed under the same historical cutoff and held-player
rules. Old absence stays missing, not a zero benchmark.

Then assemble one consistent evidence representation. A translated event profile
must carry its actual exposure, recency, source uncertainty and translation support.
Keep MLB, upper/lower affiliated minors and foreign evidence distinguishable, but
do not feed numerous duplicate raw/translated versions as unrestricted additive
talent signals. Use baseball-sized fixed units or source-noise scales rather than
scaling an almost-always-default column by its tiny variance among future MLB
participants. Fixed scaling is a regularization choice, not a claim of a known
biological stabilization threshold.

When MLB evidence accumulates, older lower-level/foreign influence must fade in
the actual forecast, not just in an unused exposure flag. Unsupported translations
remain flagged and use an explicit fallback. Keep the current positive MLB tracking
and translated-prospect branches in the comparison; do not erase their information
to make a replacement easier to fit. Avoid using fitted training predictions from
a target-trained incumbent as ordinary stacking inputs: generated supervised
inputs require nested held-player, chronological predictions or a documented
alternative that does not claim cross-fitted stacking.

For opportunity, represent actual professional activity separately from absence
of domestic statistics. A known NPB/KBO first-team season is not a rookie level,
but its PA cannot simply be relabeled as MLB PA. Known job evidence and its age
remain separate from ability and service experience. Broad OF is a known role
with unresolved specific position, not an unknown hitter. Temporary, unresolved
and permanent absence need different availability representations; a tentative
return is not an observed return. Unknown available days cannot be guessed from
the original total suspension length. Use shared ordinary return/job examples,
and show when genuine rare support still requires an uncertain fallback.

Before any fit, write one bounded exact comparison contract with the selected
feature definitions, support/routing rules and model settings. It must contrast
the unchanged current anchor, the repaired domestic representation and the same
representation plus existing foreign evidence, not a tournament or a collection
of post-result subgroup winners. Final algorithm/precision settings are not yet
sealed by this plan; derive them from source/model inventory, never from these
players' realized error improvements. Pair any additional source family with a
matched exposure/missingness control if its incremental value is claimed.

## Checks that must precede model selection

Run source-only stress checks as well as chronological, whole-player preflights:

- Add positive MLB exposure with fixed rates to a hypothetical profile. Older
  foreign/lower-level information share must decline by construction. This is a
  synthetic mechanics check, not an actual player forecast or independent win.
- Tiny old DSL/rehab evidence must have a bounded influence consistent with its
  actual precision; missing data must not be converted to poor performance.
- A broad OF role must remain known. Changing a future injury, transaction or
  season result must not change any origin-known predictor or fallback.
- Reconstruct every benchmark and candidate in the same value/reference units.
  Preserve entrants, exits and the full difficult cohort, including additions
  separately where the old forecast is absent.
- Count actual matching people for each fitted head and transformation subset.
  Coarse support or physical bounds cannot certify the rare forecast.

Score PA, probabilities where estimated, PA-weighted future MLB hitting, delivered
batting value, matched public forecasts, each origin/stage and cohort totals. Keep
the 2021 stress check but also pre-COVID and later origins. Do not rescale forecast
totals to the realized target population. Inspect whether errors improve by
cancellation between workload and rate.

After the fixed run, repeat the required source-to-forecast player walks for both
gains and harms, retaining Ohtani/Lee/Yoshida, Yordan/Benintendi, finite/uncertain
absence, rookies, ordinary hitters and non-arrivals. Cases need origin-known peers
and honest support qualifications. Actual new outcome-selected large errors join
the manifest; favorable prior cases cannot replace unfavorable ones.

## Finish the candidate rather than extending the patch queue

Choose only a coherent model supported by matched evidence and understandable
player mechanics. A negative but correctly designed repair is useful evidence;
it is not permission to declare foreign history, detailed contact or prospects
unhelpful. If representation still fails, identify the specific failing computation
before a further fit. Stop unrelated feature additions and repeated variants.

Once defensible, freeze the historical development candidate, describe rate versus
opportunity versus delivered contribution in a short model card, and expose player
traces with a team filter in a separate research explorer. State remaining defense,
valuation, population and multiyear limitations. Do not call a next-year batting
candidate a finished six-year full-WAR/trade-value system. The full active goal
cannot be marked complete while those requested practical deliverables or meaningful
benchmark/cohort/reasonability gaps remain.
