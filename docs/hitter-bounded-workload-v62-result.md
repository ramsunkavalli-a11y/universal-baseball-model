withheld after seventy saved heads replay and seventeen actual player reviews.

## What was compared

The [locked contract](hitter-bounded-workload-v62-contract.md) compares smooth
squared-error and bounded fractional-logit means on identical V61 information,
35 chronological whole-player cells and 30,506 forecasts. Both retain exits,
non-arrivals, qualified observation state and corrected V53 hitting. Zero PA
labels enter directly. The bounded mean is not an appearance probability or a
calibrated outcome distribution. Same numeric penalties do not imply equal
effective regularization across different losses.

## Why the totals matter more than a bounded output

The bounded mean never produces negative PA; smooth squared does so in 12,150
rows, all subjected to the declared zero bound. This numerical improvement
does not yield a valid allocation. Lower-minor actual PA totals are 6,072,
against 8,129 baseline, 199,534 smooth squared and 27,106 bounded. Never-debut
actual use totals 98,328 versus 339,638 smooth squared. Both new models retain
poor prospect readiness and substantial origin errors.

The actual cohort totals below are known hitters' use, not the complete league
budget or future teams. Do not force them to full MLB totals.

An [actual fitted context audit](../reports/model-evidence/hitter-bounded-workload-v62/context-term-audit.json)
finds the reorganization flag constant across all players within each origin.
It cannot vary in 2021's mature training data, so that term is dropped, as is
the newly appearing lag-one cancellation flag. This is not unseen data silently
converted to a failed season; it is an unsupported new regime.

In 2022's fits the squared model learns a positive reorganization coefficient
that adds 32.18–38.79 raw PA to every test player before clipping. In 2023 it adds
21.30–30.97. The bounded model also learns large positive calendar terms, though
its nonlinear link means they are not additive PA awards. By 2024 the squared
term instead subtracts about 9–15 PA. A shifting calendar contrast should not
be interpreted as player talent or as a reliable expansion in jobs for everyone.
The audit traces saved arithmetic; it does not prove a causal reorganization
effect or certify a counterfactual replacement forecast.

This is a consequential representation failure, not evidence against all
smooth or bounded systems. Unrestricted additive use of common calendar flags
is especially poor for a large mostly-zero prospect population. The baseline's
tree interactions do not make an identical uniform PA award. Cancellation and
reorganization still belong in source/history interpretation; their presence
does not certify proper treatment. Do not fix this by a favorable-year total
rescaling or promote a post-result subgroup blend.

## Actual player findings

The [complete walkthrough](../reports/model-evidence/hitter-bounded-workload-v62/player-walkthrough.md)
reconstructs pooled MLB/AAA K and HR independently from actual counts, traces
every saved additive predictor and link, and preserves origin-selected peers.
These include failed peers, not only eventual stars. Important cases are:

- Judge after his brief debut falls 281 baseline PA to 209 smooth or 199
  bounded versus 678 actual, despite 410 AAA PA/19 HR and scouting evidence.
  His unchanged hitting forecast also badly misses the breakout.
- Langford's top-draft evidence is present, but bounded use falls 43 to 20
  versus 557 actual. Broad age/stage support is not matched elite-entry support.
- Soto at eighteen has unusually low A-ball K, plus good prior complex
  production, yet all arms miss his next-year jump: 0.13 baseline, 4.34 smooth,
  1.10 bounded versus 494 PA. His nearest generic peers all receive zero. This
  shows the exceptional pathway gap, not a case for hundreds of next-year PA
  for every productive eighteen-year-old. Next-year use is not long-term value.
- Alford's scouting/roster evidence yields 54 bounded PA versus 71 baseline
  and eight actual, while smooth overpredicts at 146. Aggressive prospect boosts
  have genuine false-positive costs. An eight-PA realized rate is not stable talent.
- McLain drops 174 to 47 bounded versus 577; Lux drops 217 to 138 versus 487.
  Corrected roster returns do not prove recovery, but these are poor comeback
  forecasts, not evidence that the repaired injury source itself causes the loss.
- Freeman after 695 PA falls 560 to 278 bounded versus 708. His captured
  non-listing does not prove no future job; this miss accompanies the severe
  2021-origin deficit. Reorganization/new cancellation context has no mature
  training contrast at that cutoff.
- Cruz improves 288 to 415 smooth versus 521; bounded stays at 284. Judge
  after 2023 improves 527 to 622 bounded versus 704. These gains remain beside
  harms, not selected into a new hybrid.
- Acuna rises 582 to 754 smooth or 717 bounded versus 222. Later adverse
  outcomes are not origin-known facts, but near-maximum expected use amplifies
  downside. Franco's unresolved restriction still gets 599 bounded versus zero.
- Yordan and established Judge show better offense through worse PA offsetting
  an underestimated hitting rate. Dubon and Stephenson's nearly exact offense
  similarly hides separate PA/rate errors. A good product alone is insufficient.
- Duffy is classified lower minors because of eight rehab A-plus PA, despite
  prior 366/612 MLB PA. This is why stage must be read jointly with prior debut;
  he is not the same profile as never-arrived lower-minor prospects.

Some large additive role terms reflect neutral rate priors at absent exposure.
They are not observed four-PA-per-game roles, causal benefits from missing
history, or standalone feature importance. Interpret the entire predictor,
exposure and presence fields together; all terms and actual inputs are saved.

## Disposition and next milestone

Eleven focused numerical/source tests pass, all seventy solves converge, and
every saved forecast replays. These are integrity checks, not predictive success.
Only one evaluated input saturates the fixed physical-unit scale, so widespread
feature saturation does not explain the loss. Sparse profiles remain in scoring.
Historical publication vintages and existing source coverage qualifications remain.

Keep the source repair and corrected V53 as the coherent research baseline.
The workload architecture batch is closed: no penalty, floor or library sweep,
no broad comeback boost, no post-result blend. Next carry V53 opportunity into
the plan's compatible hitting-talent and delivered-value comparison. Check
reused target units first; earlier target-season values cannot be scored as
common-origin values without reconstruction. Carry the shared-calendar warning
into that representation, and keep prospect pathways, uncertainty and public
playing-time gaps explicit. Do not claim the full hitter model is finished.
