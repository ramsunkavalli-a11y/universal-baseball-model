# Test a simple arm and receiving talent baseline

The corrected source/support audit precedes this comparison. At the 2022 origin,
47 of 488 origin-eligible arm players and 18 of 246 receiving players have the
predeclared measured later MLB quality. Mixed positions, exits and inadequate
samples remain unknown quality. This selected measured population cannot certify
all-player, lower-minors or delivered-value accuracy.

Compare neutral quality with a single transparent shrunk-history recipe. Use
only valid, scope-qualified observations in the preceding three seasons with
weights 1, 0.5 and 0.25. The candidate is 100 times weighted native runs divided
by weighted actual opportunities plus 300 arm chances or 600 received throws.
These fixed neutral prior exposures roughly represent a substantial season;
they are conservative assumptions, not statistically estimated reliability or
literature-derived constants. They are not tuned after results. Small samples
reduce the actual run signal. No measured history produces a neutral fallback
with an explicit unknown-talent flag, not an observed average grade.

No learned age model: arm training at 2022 has only 55–67 distinct held-player
people, while receiving has zero mature three-year training examples. Freeze
this recipe before scoring and use no outcome-dependent player overrides.

Primary scoring is equal-player RMSE at 2022 in runs per 100 actual later
opportunities, with MAE, bias and a 2,000-draw paired person bootstrap using seed
70633647. Report all earlier complete origins without selecting the best year;
2021 is a stress cohort. Age and history-size group scores qualify the pooled
result, not micro-difference vetoes. Repeated origins are not independent people.
Totals using actual later opportunities are diagnostic only, not a forecast of
delivered runs or evidence that exposure has been modeled.

Walk the fixed source-contract cases, the two smallest history samples, the
largest gain and deterioration, largest positive/negative candidate error and
median error per component. Add three age/history-opportunity/ID-selected peers
for each and retain unknown futures. Show annual source records, actual weighting,
shrinkage, predictions and later annual contributions. Any selection category
without an eligible fixed case is reported, never filled with hindsight.

Complete the player review and independent replay before disposition. Retain
useful qualified research evidence whether gains are certain or inconclusive;
do not claim full WAR improvement. No prior sweep or algorithm tournament follows
this test. Next work remains position/opportunity forecasting and matched value
integration, with explicit fallback/transport limits. The selected forecast,
explorer and 2026 evaluation remain unchanged.
