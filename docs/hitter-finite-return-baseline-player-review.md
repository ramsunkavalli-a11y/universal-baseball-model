# Player checks for the finite return role reference

The Tatis source-to-forecast mechanism works, but the same fitted role reference
has serious other failures. The finite-budget candidate changes only Tatis before
2023. The numbers below distinguish that candidate from reference forecasts
which are diagnostics and have NOT replaced other players' predictions.

All outcomes are next-year MLB PA, through 2025. Values are batting plus replacement,
not full WAR. Ten fixed diagnostic rows precede fitting; five further rows expose
the largest reference gain/harm, false high/low and an ordinary case. Players with
zero subsequent PA remain included. The machine-readable walks preserve actual
three-year statistics, inputs, coefficient contributions, source records and
origin-feature-selected peers. Peers are not matched on medical recovery.

Tatis: the source record retains 546 MLB PA and 42 HR in 2021, plus 257 PA and
17 HR in shortened 2020. Their seasonal normalization is unchanged. The medical
report restores known surgery, but does not certify health. The conditional
ready-reference is 690.57 PA, and one 142/162 suspension adjustment gives 605.12
expected PA. Without removing the gap it gives 520.32 after suspension.
That conditional contrast is explicit, not selected after seeing his 635 actual.
His hitting stays 1.313 batting units per 600 PA; contribution rises .327 to
3.219 versus 2.757 actual, so the better workload still overstates contribution.

There is no same-origin established/linked absent peer for Tatis. The actual-gap
training profile has one distinct person; its transported ordinary-role profile
has 90, but its surgery/high-medical-absence intersection has zero. The additive
completion review selects ordinary-role comparisons by the same age, past role
and quality distance, while explicitly relaxing only the interruption category.
They illustrate the assumed role, not his medical prognosis.

Hoskins: known prior 672 PA and 30 HR support an established role. The reference
gives 99.1% appearance times 447.7 conditional PA, but preserves a gap penalty.
It is closer than 367 to his actual 517, yet does not apply because he has no
remaining suspension budget. Lux and Stassi are origin-selected absent/linked
comparisons: they later get 487 and zero, respectively. Their different outcomes
show why a common medical interruption does not guarantee recovery or workload.

Ellsbury: his prior MLB seasons have 626 and 409 PA. The report at the January
cutoff anticipated spring recovery. His later February setback is unavailable
then. The reference's 98.8% appearance is not a credible quantified medical
confidence given zero exact training profiles; 344 PA is not adopted. His later
zero cannot justify erasing the prior expectation with hindsight.

Kang and Franco: talent and reported organizational links are not legal clearance.
The reference's 314 and 532 PA are inapplicable role diagnostics, not their new
forecasts. Both fail the finite-return gate. Franco's existing 545 remains an
unresolved major forecast failure; leaving it unchanged does not validate it.
Marcano's permanent exclusion remains zero. Do not interpret reference numbers
computed outside eligibility as supported forecasts.

Duran: his completed restriction is not charged again. A near-identical 584
reference supports an ordinary role comparison, not a solved 696-PA forecast.

Belt: his 404-PA/19-HR preceding season supports some work despite being unsigned;
the reference's 198 is close to the existing 195. Origin-selected Martinez,
Solano and Duvall subsequently play, while Belt does not. His surprising zero
is not a reason to force all unsigned productive veterans to zero.

Judge: 704 PA and 58 HR in 2024 support a regular job, but the reference grants
large positive workload effects for his exceptional hitting. It predicts 735
versus 679 actual. More batting quality need not buy more than an everyday role.
Ordinary-role support is plentiful; role saturation remains a structural limit.

## Cases selected to expose reference errors

McLain before 2024 is the largest reference gain: 486 versus the benchmark's
598, before zero actual. His known 2023 MLB debut had 403 PA and 16 HR. A lower
forecast improving his eventual zero does not mean the later interruption was
predicted or justify a preseason zero. The candidate leaves him at 598.

Travis before 2017 is the largest harm: 800 versus 530, before 197 actual. His
known 2016 MLB line has 432 PA, 11 HR and 87 strikeouts. Recent medical inputs
add roughly 434 PA to the centered reference; raw conditional PA reaches 851
before clipping. These fitted associations do not imply injuries improve health.
The resulting forecast fails baseball reasonability regardless of pooled score.

Tatis before 2022 is the largest false high: 652 versus 556, before zero actual.
The 2022 injury and suspension had not happened at that January cutoff. This
review cannot use them as predictors. The miss is not the same source failure
as the 2023 forecast, when the suspension and recovery expectation WERE known.

Moustakas before 2018 is the largest false low: 119 versus 443, before 635 actual.
The preceding 598 PA and 38 HR remain in the role inputs. Conditional workload is
412; only 28.8% participation drives the low forecast because his employment
state is unsigned/released. Five matching training people cannot support that
near-exit interpretation confidently. Morrison, Nunez and Gonzalez are
origin-selected comparisons with later positive workload.

Sanchez before 2019 is the ordinary near-match: 446 reference PA versus 446 actual.
Known prior MLB workload is 229, 525 and 374 PA. The benchmark gives 516. This
useful case does not erase the Travis/Moustakas failures or certify the health
coefficients. His selected peers include both useful contributors and Hanson,
who later gets only 48 PA; the comparison selection was outcome-blind.

All fifteen cases are reviewed. The reference is rejected as a broad replacement;
Tatis's conditional reconstruction remains provisional, with full clinical
calibration still open. No player forecast or explorer is promoted.
