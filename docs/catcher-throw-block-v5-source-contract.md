# Historical catcher throwing and blocking measurement checks

2026-10-06. Catcher skill needs actual opportunity denominators and difficulty
adjustment. First recover the public MLB throwing and blocking records with
explicit years and a one-opportunity minimum, preserving old sources and fits.
This source checkpoint does not fit a talent model or reopen the flawed narrative
blocking or deterrence experiments.

## Measurement and scope

Capture regular-season individual catchers, unsplit all-team totals, throwing
to all available destination bases. Start with 2022 and 2025 for both channels.
If their year metadata, identities, denominators and native-run reconciliation
pass, extend that exact query to throwing 2016–2025 and blocking 2018–2025.
Never query 2026. Keep raw responses immutable and stop on a stale/default year.

Use caught-stealing above average and actual tracked steal attempts for throwing;
use blocks above average and the source's actual blocking opportunities for
blocking. Confirm what its `pitches` field means rather than assuming it equals
the framing denominator. Compare source run values against the independently
captured native position ledger. Missing attempts, zero innings or undefined
rates do not measure average ability. Do not apportion unmatched residual runs.

MLB's [throwing description](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/catcher-throwing?game_type=Regular&n=1&season_end=2025&season_start=2025&split=no&target_base=All&team=&type=Cat&with_team_only=1)
adjusts steal outcomes for the opportunity presented, including the runner and
pitch circumstances; its stated conversion is 0.65 runs per additional caught
stealing. This is success given attempts, not independent prevention of attempts.
The [blocking description](https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/leaderboard/catcher-blocking?season_end=2025&season_start=2025)
expresses difficulty-adjusted blocks in runs at 0.25 per block. Neither source
certifies identical tracking for the lower minors.

[Carruth and Jensen's throwing study](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.3257) uses
opportunities and hierarchical shrinkage across seasons, motivating explicit
sample-size regression rather than ranking on raw caught-stealing percentage.
Those principles do not supply the current metric's reliability coefficient.

## Required evidence before the next comparison

Report per-season people and exposures, canonical payload uniqueness, requested
and observed year metadata, reconciliation gaps and any change of definition.
Trace fixed catchers Realmuto, Sánchez, Hedges, Raleigh, Kirk and Bailey when
eligible, plus the two smallest positive samples per channel and season. Select
three peers using only origin exposure and player ID. Show counts, numerator,
raw rate and native run conversion; absence stays unknown. A full-history source
extension must receive its own annual coverage and player review.

Then count mature three-calendar-year future MLB quality windows and actual
cutoff-known held-player training support before locking a separate talent
comparison. A source pass is not predictive success, workload prediction, WAR
integration or deployment approval. The selected hitter forecast, explorer and
completed 2026 evaluation remain unchanged.
