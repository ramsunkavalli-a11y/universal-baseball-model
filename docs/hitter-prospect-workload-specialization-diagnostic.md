# Separating workload bias from arrival allocation

2026-10-04. Post-fit explanatory diagnostic within the required player review,
not a new experiment, fitted forecast or selection rule. The comparison remains
provisional until the player review is completed.

Initial scores show the unchanged conditional model's summed PA among actual
first-time MLB entrants is close to their observed total, despite low forecasts
for several stars. Check the exact retrospective accounting before assuming
all conditional workloads need an upward correction.

For each saved arm, its total expected-PA error equals conditional forecast
error among actual entrants, minus the probability discount on those entrants,
plus PA allocated to players who did not arrive. Save these three terms for all
prospects, stages, origins, rank bands, thin histories and new draftees. Future
arrival is used only to explain errors; it never changes forecasts or training.
This identity is descriptive and does not causally identify a single bad head.

Also inspect the unchanged forecast in fixed probability bins separated at
.01, .10, .50 and .80, and conditional-mean PA bins separated at 100, 200 and
400. Show all observations, predicted versus actual arrival frequencies, and
conditional forecasts versus actual PA among entrants. These diagnostics are
raw summed or pooled quantities, not the equal-year primary loss. They can
reveal offsetting allocation errors; a near-exact conditional sum is not proof
of individual or subgroup calibration. No recalibration, upward multiplier,
feature change or new fit is authorized by this diagnostic.
