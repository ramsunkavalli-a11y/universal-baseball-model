# Tiny blocking coverage gaps remain unknown

2026-10-06, before talent fitting. The 2022 and 2025 throwing numerators and
the exact blocking numerator reconcile with native runs for every captured row.
Blocking uses `x_pbwp - n_pbwp`, not its rounded displayed block or run value;
its rate per game is that numerator divided by `pitches`, multiplied by 40.
The denominator is the source's blocking chances, not framing's received pitches.

The 2025 blocking export omits Chad Wallach despite six native catcher outs and
+0.002331 blocking runs. The matching HTML page confirms the requested 2025 year
and `tot_n=1`, but also omits him. Why that tiny sample is omitted remains
unresolved. Changing `n` to zero does not recover him: that export reverts to
qualified coverage with 65 rows, versus 110 at `n=1`. Never use this zero-minimum
response as an all-player source.

This gap prevents a claim of complete all-catcher blocking coverage, not use of
the 110 reconciled records. Keep the missing record and any comparable extension
gaps in the coverage ledger with unknown opportunities/quality. Do not infer
zero pitches, zero skill or apportion the nonzero residual. Any future quality
window with positive native catcher exposure and a missing channel measurement
is unknown. This rule precedes future-window counting or performance comparison;
no inconvenient future prediction is removed after scoring.

The same year-specific capture may extend to the contracted historical years.
Its source review must certify captured measurements separately from coverage
and keep every gap visible. This qualifies the original complete-coverage hope;
it does not repair the older PBP extractor, validate MLB talent or authorize
forecast/explorer deployment.
