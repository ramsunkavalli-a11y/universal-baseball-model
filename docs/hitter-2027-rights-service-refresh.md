# Current rights and service refresh contract

October 9, 2026, before creating new control tables. No player-value fit.

Reuse the captured October 9 official roster/people/transaction sources and
the stable-ID accepted opening-2026 service balances in the older financial
snapshot. Do not reuse its September service totals or Phase 1 forecasts.
Resolve all 4,851 forecast identities, including players not on current rosters.
An explicit election of free agency ends rights; a generic designation for
assignment does not. Preserve unresolved owners and historical contract costs.

Reconstruct regular-season service separately from PA. MLB active/IL/paid-list
days count; capped at 172 per season. The official season endpoint supplies
the window. National-team and All-Star activations cannot end MLB service.
Likewise, a minor team's activation cannot silently cancel an MLB option or IL
state. Restrict automatic service state changes to direct MLB transactions;
retain other events for audit. A release/election ends the current state, not
the guaranteed contract liability. Ambiguous suspension/retirement/list events
remain review cases instead of assuming their service rules.

Roster-entry status can describe how an interval ENDED (Bailey's former club
row says Traded), so it cannot always reconstruct the season-opening state.
Where the current roster-entry adapter has no specific opening state, use the
latest conclusive pre-opening MLB transaction, preserving its provenance.
Track intervening unresolved events and incomplete years. Do not equate a
missing opening state with zero service. Existing zero-debut logic is only a
zero opening balance, not proof of zero current MLB IL service.

status), Eldridge (option, call-up, bereavement and IL), current free agents,
