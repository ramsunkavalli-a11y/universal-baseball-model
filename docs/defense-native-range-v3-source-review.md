# Native defense ledger: what is usable

2026-10-06, before fitting. The extraction recovers 13,304 non-pitcher
player-position-season rows and 13,402 origin-position candidates. Native
range, arms, double plays, first-base receiving and catcher components add back
to total runs within 2.2e-14 and recompose across positions to the same-vintage
player components within 2.9e-14. Independent official exposure qualification is retained.

This does NOT certify every aggregate innings total from summing position rows.
The public splits omit some tiny positions: 80–120 player aggregate totals per
season differ, generally by a few innings. Those discrepancies are preserved
in the source report. Only independently qualified actual position exposure is
used for range quality; aggregate innings are not apportioned across missing
positions. Missing metrics are not zero-filled for labels or histories.

First-base receiving starts in 2021 and catcher blocking in 2018 in these files.
Earlier totals therefore contain fewer measured components. Do not compare
those totals as if measurement were constant. The ledger also has small arm
credits at infield positions, and a tiny catcher arm credit for Grandal in 2024;
do not force all arm credits into an outfield bucket or label them catcher
steal throwing. Catcher pitch/attempt denominators are still not certified here.

## Origin-known evidence traced to later range

All figures below are range runs per 500 defensive innings, not WAR. Histories
use the locked three-year recency weights and 3,000-out prior. The source trace
has all actual annual positions, including those not used by the stated-position
estimate. The following examples are fixed diagnostic names, not a validation
sample chosen to flatter the baseline.

- Kiermaier, 2022 CF: 6.75 weighted range runs in 3,052 weighted outs produce
  +1.67 after shrinkage. Later CF quality is +5.80 over 2023–2025; two measured
  seasons suffice despite no third-year exposure. Origin-selected peers Springer,
  Hicks and Bradley have insufficient same-position quality, not zero talent.
- Castellanos, 2022 RF: −12.91 weighted runs/5,062.75 outs yield −2.40. Future
  quality is −2.94; all three annual RF observations are negative. Peers Renfroe,
  Judge and Grichuk have measured quality and differing outcomes.
- Arenado, 2022 3B: +15.65/5,637 yields +2.72; future quality is +2.12. His
  older, positive history does not require an exceptional young-player rule.
- Semien, 2022 2B: +7.93/5,748.5 yields +1.36, versus later +3.76. His earlier
  negative SS record is retained but not confused with measured 2B quality.
  Peers Wong, Leury García and Muncy have unknown same-position future quality.
- Witt, 2022 SS: rookie −6.88/2,477 yields −1.89, while later SS quality is
  +4.89. This is a genuine development miss for a history-only estimate. His
  3B record is separate; Luis García, Perdomo and Livan Soto are age/exposure
  comparisons selected before using future outcomes.
- Ezequiel Tovar, 2022 SS: −0.97 runs in just 234 outs yields −0.45, while
  later quality is +3.53. Heavy shrinkage limits the negative cameo's influence
  but cannot identify his eventual talent without more evidence. The fixed
  surname selection also retains Wilfredo Tovar, whose future quality is unknown;
  it does not silently substitute the successful player.
