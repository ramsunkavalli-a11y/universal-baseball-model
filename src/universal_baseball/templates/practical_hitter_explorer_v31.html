Matched benchmark results and important limits

Headline errors give each forecast year equal weight. Steamer comparisons use the same 1,789 active-origin forecasts, including 339 players with zero PA next year. Public preseason archives may contain later roster/injury information than our December cutoff. Converted public batting value is not official WAR and has an environment-mapping caveat.

Raw hitting counts are separated by league and shrunk toward fixed priors, but this rebuild does not yet include park-neutral or opponent-adjusted contact data. Mexican League hitting is kept separate from affiliated AAA. Older source affiliations can be missing; we do not substitute a player's future organization. Injuries, foreign performance, and indefinite availability are not comprehensively modeled.
