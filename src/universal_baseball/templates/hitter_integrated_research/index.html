Hitting /600 isolates expected MLB hitting above league average per 600 PA, in custom win units. It has no position bonus. Batting contribution adds replacement and scales by expected PA; it is not full WAR. A player with no later MLB PA has no observed hitting rate. Rate errors use the later season's league reference; contribution outcomes use a fixed origin reference, so the two actual columns do not directly multiply into one another.
