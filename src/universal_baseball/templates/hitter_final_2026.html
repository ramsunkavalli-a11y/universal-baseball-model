How to read the numbers and limits

Hit / 600 is conditional next-year MLB hitting above average, with neutral event weights. Expected batting = expected PA × (Hit / 600 ÷ 600 + 570 ÷ 182,926). Actual batting uses completed 2026 hitting relative to its league and the same fixed replacement reference. Neither includes defense, running or position. Error is forecast minus actual.

Rate accuracy is weighted by actual PA among players who appeared. All-player delivered-value error includes non-arrivals; it is not a regular player's typical error. Zero-PA players have zero delivered contribution, but no observed hitting rate. Sparse warnings mean fewer than 20 distinct training people in at least one declared joint profile. They are not confidence intervals.

Past exposure pooling uses weights 1, 0.8, 0.6 for 2025, 2024, 2023. Level translation is not a complete park-neutral major-league equivalent. Scouting enters both workload and prospect hitting. Measured MLB players also use exit velocity and launch-angle evidence; untracked players retain the numeric route. No new models were fitted after outcomes opened.

Player walks show exact arithmetic. Individual tree paths and correlated regression terms are not causal effects. Very young promotion cameos, foreign entrants, full defense and multi-year control/trade value remain unresolved. This season cannot be reused as a protected test after further changes.
