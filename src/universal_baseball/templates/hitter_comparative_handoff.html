What changed—and what is still weak

The candidate uses fresher preseason prospect rankings. Hitting estimates are unchanged: three years of batting counts separated by level, age and MLB history, with recent seasons weighted more and small samples shrunk toward a prior. Separate appearance and PA-if-active models use performance, workload, draft pedigree, rankings, games and role evidence.

Graduation and employment additions were reviewed but not adopted. On 2,627 public matches the candidate's average playing-time error is 106.41 PA versus Steamer's 92.08: 15.56% worse, outside our 15% working target. Upper-minor readiness improves, but Kurtz and other rapid arrivals remain badly missed. Unusual returns, extreme stars, historical source dating and incomplete foreign/signing evidence remain weaknesses. This branch is not certified park-neutral; defense and baserunning are absent. Support is not certainty, and no calibrated continuous prediction intervals are available.

Stage Review flags

Hitting /600 means custom-event batting wins above an average MLB hitter over 600 PA. It excludes position, defense and running. For non-MLB players it is an uncertain conditional MLB extrapolation, not a prospect grade. An average hitter is zero on this rate scale; replacement is added only to expected offense.
