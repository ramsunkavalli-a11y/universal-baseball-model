Position and playing time are not yet jointly reconciled. A direct position estimate can imply more or less defensive use than the separately displayed PA forecast. The breakdown makes those disagreements visible; it is not a unified simulation of the player's season.

What this model is built on—and what remains missing

Batting: the existing Year-1 five-model ensemble combines direct and participation-based forecasts from LightGBM, XGBoost, Explainable Boosting Machine and ridge regression. It uses age, level, playing-time history, ordinary batting results and detailed contact evidence where covered. Missing-data players use the existing fallback. Years 2–3 retain the previously selected direct ridge forecasts. These batting means have not changed here.

Playing time: the existing all-level opportunity forecast remains in place, including the accepted recent-MLB-history probability update for established hitters. It accounts for no MLB playing time. It is still estimated separately from batting value; do not divide batting value by expected PA to infer hitting talent.

New component layer: dated position transitions, the previously successful steal/advancement recipes, and heavily regressed historical native MLB defensive runs compete with a fixed direct ridge model. Each historical model choice uses earlier completed tests only. There is no new algorithm tournament or hand-picked blending weight.

Evidence limits: the full three-year comparison has only two normal starting years once blocking coverage is required. General defense is much less informed for minor leaguers; old MiLB range and battery translations have not earned inclusion. Some catcher contributions are uncertain, and later-year neutral estimates mean “no supported adjustment,” not “this player has no skill.” First-base receiving and a separate double-play-avoidance running component are omitted. Published defensive runs are noisy estimates.

Framing: the historical scenario is not a calibrated prediction of challenge-ABS effects. The zero-framing option removes framing only, as a full-ABS sensitivity. It does not assume full ABS arrives in any particular year. Throwing and blocking stay separate.

No 2026 outcomes were used. The original frozen forecast remains unchanged. Confidence ranges are withheld rather than displayed with unsupported precision. Units use 10 runs per win; this component ledger is not an exact reproduction of FanGraphs WAR.
